---
title: Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies
date: 2026-08-03 03:00:03 -0400
description: A straightforward creamed-butter dough with an extra yolk and no fridge rest — the everyday chocolate chip cookie, not an upgraded one.
image: /assets/images/Chewy-Chocolate-Chip-Cookies.webp
servings: 24
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [dessert, baking, quick]
ingredients:
  - 2¼ cups | flour
  - 1 tsp | baking soda
  - 1 tsp | salt
  - 1 cup | unsalted butter, softened
  - 1 cup | brown sugar, packed
  - ½ cup | granulated sugar
  - 1 | egg
  - 1 | egg yolk
  - 2 tsp | vanilla extract
  - 2 cups | semisweet chocolate chips
method:
  - Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl and set aside.
  - Beat the softened butter with the brown sugar and granulated sugar until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  - Beat in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla until fully combined.
  - Add the dry ingredients and mix on low just until no streaks of flour remain.
  - Fold in the chocolate chips.
  - Scoop into 2-tablespoon mounds onto parchment-lined sheet pans, spaced 2 inches apart.
  - Heat the oven to 375°F and bake until the edges are golden and set but the centers still look pale and slightly underdone, 9 to 11 minutes.
  - Let cool on the pan for 5 minutes before moving to a rack.
---

The extra yolk and the lean toward brown sugar over white are what keep the center chewy instead of cakey — egg whites dry a cookie out, and brown sugar's molasses holds onto moisture that granulated can't. Pull the pan the moment the edges are set and the middle still looks underdone; they finish cooking on the hot sheet as they cool, and that's the last minute you don't want to spend in the oven.

No fridge rest needed here, so it's a same-day cookie if you want one — mixer to oven in under an hour. Scoop extra dough into balls and freeze them; bake straight from frozen, adding a minute or two.
