---
title: One-Pan Chicken Alfredo
date: 2026-08-01 13:00:03 -0400
description: Seared chicken and fettuccine finished together in a cream-Parmesan sauce, no second pot for the pasta water.
image: /assets/images/One-Pan-Chicken-Alfredo.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [chicken, pasta, quick, main, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | chicken breasts, sliced thin
  - Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  - 2 tbsp | olive oil
  - 3 tbsp | butter
  - 4 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 2½ cups | chicken broth
  - 1½ cups | heavy cream
  - 8 oz | fettuccine
  - 1 cup | grated Parmesan
  - To finish | chopped parsley, extra Parmesan
method:
  - Season the chicken with salt and pepper and sear in olive oil over medium-high heat until golden and cooked through, about 3 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
  - Melt the butter in the same pan, add the garlic, and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Pour in the broth and cream, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan, and bring to a simmer.
  - Add the fettuccine, pressing it down until submerged, and cook uncovered, stirring often, until the pasta is tender and the sauce coats the back of a spoon, 12 to 14 minutes.
  - Stir in the Parmesan until melted and the sauce turns glossy.
  - Slice the chicken and return it to the pan with any accumulated juices, tossing to coat.
  - Finish with parsley and more Parmesan before serving.
---

The pasta cooks straight in the cream and broth, so its starch is what thickens the sauce — no separate pot, no roux, and no risk of the sauce breaking the way a cream sauce built apart from the pasta sometimes does. Keep the heat at a real simmer rather than a boil, or the cream can split before the fettuccine is done.

Swap in bone-in chicken thighs for more forgiving, harder-to-overcook meat, and adjust the sear time up a few minutes accordingly. Leftovers reheat fine with a splash of broth to loosen the sauce back up.
