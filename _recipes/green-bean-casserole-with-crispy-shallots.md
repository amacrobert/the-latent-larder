---
title: Green Bean Casserole with Crispy Shallots
date: 2026-08-01 02:00:03 -0400
description: The Thanksgiving standby rebuilt without a can of soup — real mushroom cream sauce under shallots fried crisp instead of tinned.
image: /assets/images/Green-Bean-Casserole-With-Crispy-Shallots.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 25 min
cook_time: 35 min
tags: [side, vegetarian, baking, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | green beans, trimmed and halved
  - 3 | shallots, thinly sliced into rings
  - ¼ cup | all-purpose flour, plus 3 tbsp
  - 1 cup | vegetable oil, for frying
  - 4 tbsp | butter
  - 8 oz | cremini mushrooms, sliced
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1½ cups | chicken or vegetable broth
  - 1 cup | heavy cream
  - ½ tsp | ground nutmeg
  - Salt and pepper | to taste
method:
  - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and blanch the green beans 4 to 5 minutes, until bright green and just tender. Drain and shock in ice water, then drain again.
  - Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Toss the shallot rings in ¼ cup flour, shake off the excess, and fry in batches until deeply golden and crisp, about 3 minutes a batch. Drain on paper towels and salt right away.
  - Pour off all but 2 tbsp of the oil. Melt the butter in the same skillet, add the mushrooms, and cook until browned and their liquid has cooked off, about 8 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Sprinkle in the remaining 3 tbsp flour and stir until it coats the mushrooms.
  - Whisk in the broth and cream. Simmer, stirring, until the sauce is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, 4 to 5 minutes. Stir in the nutmeg and season with salt and pepper.
  - Fold the blanched green beans into the sauce and transfer everything to a greased 2-quart baking dish.
  - Bake at 375°F until bubbling at the edges, about 20 minutes. Scatter the crispy shallots over the top and bake 2 to 3 minutes more, just to set them, before serving.
---

The can of cream of mushroom soup is the whole reason this dish has a reputation for tasting like a can of cream of mushroom soup. A real béchamel-style sauce built off actual mushrooms fixes that without changing anything about the dish people expect to see on the table.

Assemble the casserole through the beans-in-sauce step up to a day ahead and refrigerate; fry the shallots separately and re-crisp them in a 375°F oven for a couple of minutes right before serving so they go on the table hot.
