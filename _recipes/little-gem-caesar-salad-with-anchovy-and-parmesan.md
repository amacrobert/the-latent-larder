---
title: Little Gem Caesar Salad with Anchovy and Parmesan
date: 2026-07-29 02:03:19 -0400
description: A raw egg yolk and a mashed anchovy whisked into a dressing sharper than anything from a bottle, tossed with lettuce that still has some crunch left in it.
image: /assets/images/Little-Gem-Caesar-Salad-With-Anchovy-And-Parmesan.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 5 min
tags: [salad, quick, side, appetizer]
ingredients:
  - 2 heads | Little Gem lettuce, torn
  - 2 cups | day-old bread, cubed
  - ¼ cup | olive oil, for croutons
  - ¼ cup | olive oil, for the dressing
  - 2 cloves | garlic
  - 4 fillets | oil-packed anchovies
  - 1 | egg yolk
  - 2 tbsp | lemon juice
  - 1 tsp | Dijon mustard
  - ½ cup | grated Parmesan, divided
  - To taste | black pepper
method:
  - Toss the bread with olive oil and toast in a skillet over medium heat, tossing often, until golden and crisp on every side.
  - Mash the garlic and anchovy fillets on a cutting board with the flat of a knife until they form a paste.
  - Whisk the paste with the egg yolk, lemon juice, and mustard until the mixture turns pale and starts to thicken.
  - Stream in the remaining olive oil a few drops at a time, whisking constantly, until the dressing is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  - Stir in half the Parmesan.
  - Toss the lettuce with the dressing until every leaf is coated.
  - Scatter the croutons and remaining Parmesan over top, finish with black pepper, and serve right away, before the leaves wilt.
---

The raw yolk is what makes this cling to the lettuce instead of pooling at the bottom of the bowl — it's the same emulsion as mayonnaise, just thinner. Don't skip mashing the anchovies into a paste first; whole fillets in the dressing give you a mouthful of fish instead of the background savoriness the salad actually wants.

If raw egg makes you nervous, a pasteurized yolk whisks exactly the same way. Top it with grilled chicken or shrimp and it stops being a side.
