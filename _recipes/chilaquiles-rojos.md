---
title: Chilaquiles Rojos with Fried Eggs and Cotija
date: 2026-08-06 21:00:03 -0400
description: Tortilla chips simmered in a charred red chile sauce until just softened, topped with a fried egg and crumbled cotija.
image: /assets/images/Chilaquiles-Rojos.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [breakfast, mexican, vegetarian, gluten-free, quick]
ingredients:
  - 3 | dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  - 2 | dried ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
  - 1 lb | plum tomatoes
  - ½ | white onion, plus more thinly sliced to finish
  - 2 cloves | garlic, unpeeled
  - 1½ cups | chicken broth
  - 3 tbsp | vegetable oil, divided
  - 8 cups | tortilla chips
  - To taste | salt
  - 4 | large eggs
  - To finish | crumbled cotija
  - To finish | Mexican crema
  - To finish | cilantro leaves
  - 1 | lime, cut into wedges
method:
  - Toast the guajillo and ancho chiles in a dry skillet over medium heat until fragrant and pliable, about 30 seconds per side, then cover with hot water and soak 15 minutes.
  - Meanwhile, char the tomatoes, onion, and garlic under the broiler, turning once, until blackened in spots, about 8 minutes.
  - Peel the garlic, then blend it with the charred tomatoes and onion, the drained chiles, and the broth until smooth.
  - Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over medium, pour in the sauce, and simmer until it coats the back of a spoon, about 10 minutes. Season with salt.
  - Fry the eggs in a separate pan with the remaining oil to your liking, whites set and yolks soft, and set aside.
  - Add the tortilla chips to the simmering sauce and toss gently until they soften at the edges but still hold some bite at the center, about 1 minute.
  - Divide among plates, top each with a fried egg, cotija, crema, sliced onion, and cilantro, and serve at once with lime wedges.
---

The one variable that matters is time in the sauce — a chip left too long goes from just-coated to wet paper, and there's no fixing it once it's there. Pull the pan off heat the moment the edges give and the centers still have resistance; the residual heat finishes the job on the walk to the table.

Skip the fry step with a bag of thick-cut, unsalted tortilla chips — they hold up in the sauce better than thin ones anyway. Shredded rotisserie chicken folded in with the chips turns this into chilaquiles con pollo.
