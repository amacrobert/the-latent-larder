---
title: Sausage and Herb Bread Stuffing
date: 2026-07-31 23:30:04 -0400
description: Cubed bread dried out and soaked in sausage fat and stock, baked until the top shatters and the center stays custardy.
servings: 8
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 50 min
tags: [bread, pork, side, baking, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1 loaf | day-old bread, torn into cubes
  - 1 lb | breakfast sausage, casings removed
  - 6 tbsp | butter
  - 1 | onion, diced
  - 3 stalks | celery, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - ¼ cup | fresh sage and thyme, chopped
  - 2 | eggs
  - 2 cups | chicken stock
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
method:
  - Spread the bread cubes on a sheet pan and dry them in a 300°F oven for 20 minutes, until they've lost their give and sound hollow when tapped.
  - Brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles, until no pink remains, about 8 minutes.
  - Add the butter, onion, and celery to the same skillet and cook until the onion turns translucent, about 6 minutes.
  - Stir in the garlic, sage, and thyme and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Whisk the eggs into the stock, then toss the bread, sausage mixture, and stock together in a large bowl until every cube is evenly damp.
  - Season with salt and pepper, pile into a buttered baking dish, and bake uncovered at 375°F for 30 minutes, until the top is deeply browned and craggy.
---

The bread has to actually be stale, or dried hard in the oven — fresh bread soaks up the stock and turns to paste before it ever sees the oven. Tear it a day ahead if you remember, or dry it low and slow the same day; either way you want it dense enough to soak without collapsing.

Assemble the whole thing the day before and hold it covered in the fridge, then bake straight from cold — add five minutes to the covered time and it comes out the same. Leave it uncovered the whole bake if you want a drier, craggier top; a foil cover for the first half keeps the center softer.
