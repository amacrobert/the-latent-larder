---
title: Chilled Cucumber and Yogurt Soup
date: 2026-07-26 00:45:05 -0400
description: A blender soup for nights too hot to stand at the stove, sharp with dill and garlic.
image: /assets/images/Chilled-Cucumber-And-Yogurt-Soup.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 1 hr chill
tags: [soup, vegetarian, quick, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2 lb | cucumbers, peeled and seeded
  - 2 cups | plain whole-milk yogurt
  - ½ cup | cold water
  - 2 cloves | garlic
  - 2 tbsp | olive oil, plus more to finish
  - 1 tbsp | white wine vinegar
  - 1 bunch | dill, plus more to finish
  - Walnuts, toasted and chopped, to finish
  - Salt, to taste
method:
  - Roughly chop the cucumbers and blend with the yogurt, water, garlic, olive oil, vinegar, and dill until completely smooth.
  - Season with salt, starting light — the flavor sharpens as it chills.
  - Refrigerate at least 1 hour, until cold straight through.
  - Taste again before serving and adjust salt and vinegar; cold food always needs more seasoning than it did at room temperature.
  - Ladle into bowls and finish with a drizzle of olive oil, more dill, and the toasted walnuts.
---

Seed the cucumbers or the soup turns watery and separates in the bowl. If your
cucumbers taste bitter, that's a sign to peel more aggressively rather than
adding more sugar to compensate.

This keeps in the fridge for a couple of days and only gets better as the
garlic mellows. Thin any leftovers with a splash of cold water before serving,
since the yogurt firms up overnight.
