---
title: Sheet-Pan Chicken with Lemon and Olives
date: 2026-07-25 23:44:56 -0400
description: Bone-in thighs roasted hard until the skin shatters, with olives and lemon doing the sauce's job.
image: /assets/images/Sheet-Pan-Chicken-With-Lemon-And-Olives.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 45 min
tags: [chicken, roasted, main]
ingredients:
  - 8 | chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
  - 2 | lemons, one sliced, one juiced
  - 1 cup | green olives, pitted
  - 3 tbsp | olive oil
  - 4 cloves | garlic, smashed
  - 1 tsp | dried oregano
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
  - A handful | parsley, chopped, to finish
method:
  - Heat the oven to 425°F.
  - Pat the chicken thighs dry and season all over with salt and pepper.
  - Toss the thighs, lemon slices, olives, and garlic with the olive oil and oregano on a sheet pan, then arrange skin-side up in a single layer.
  - Roast until the skin is deeply browned and blistered and the juices run clear, 40 to 45 minutes.
  - Rest the chicken on the pan for 5 minutes, then spoon the pan juices and lemon over the top.
  - Squeeze the juiced lemon over everything and scatter with parsley just before serving.
---

The skin is the whole point, so don't crowd the pan — thighs touching each other steam instead of crisping. If you only have boneless thighs, cut the roasting time by a third and check them early; they'll brown faster and dry out if you leave them to the full 45 minutes.

The olives are doing the seasoning work a pan sauce usually would, so taste one before you add extra salt. Leftovers are good cold, picked off the bone and tossed through a green salad with a spoonful of the lemony pan juices.
