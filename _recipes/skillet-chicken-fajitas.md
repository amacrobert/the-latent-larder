---
title: Skillet Chicken Fajitas with Charred Peppers
date: 2026-08-04 19:00:03 -0400
description: Lime-and-chili marinated chicken thighs seared hard in a cast-iron skillet with peppers and onions until they hit an audible sizzle.
image: /assets/images/Skillet-Chicken-Fajitas.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [chicken, quick, main, spicy]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | boneless, skinless chicken thighs, sliced into strips
  - 3 tbsp | lime juice
  - 3 tbsp | olive oil, divided
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp | chili powder
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | smoked paprika
  - To taste | salt
  - 2 | bell peppers, sliced
  - 1 | onion, sliced
  - 8 | small flour tortillas, warmed
  - To finish | lime wedges and cilantro
method:
  - Toss the chicken with the lime juice, 2 tablespoons of the oil, garlic, chili powder, cumin, paprika, and salt; let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes.
  - Heat a cast-iron or other heavy skillet over high heat until it just begins to smoke.
  - Lay the chicken in a single layer and sear undisturbed until deeply browned on one side, about 3 minutes, then flip and cook until no longer pink in the center, 3 to 4 minutes more; transfer to a plate.
  - Add the remaining oil, peppers, and onion to the same skillet and cook over high heat, stirring occasionally, until charred at the edges and just tender, about 6 minutes.
  - Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the skillet and toss until sizzling, about 1 minute.
  - Serve immediately in the warm tortillas with lime wedges and cilantro.
---

Let the chicken sit untouched for that first three minutes — a crowded pan that gets stirred early steams instead of charring, and the char is the whole point of cooking this in a skillet instead of the oven. The fond it leaves behind is what seasons the peppers.

Swap in sliced flank steak for the chicken if that's what's on hand; sear it the same way, just pull it a shade earlier since it's better rare than well-done.
