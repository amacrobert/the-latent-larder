---
title: Crispy Baked Chicken Tenders with Honey Mustard
date: 2026-08-02 07:00:02 -0400
description: Buttermilk-soaked and double-dredged in panko, baked hot on a wire rack to crisp on every side without a drop of frying oil.
image: /assets/images/Crispy-Baked-Chicken-Tenders-With-Honey-Mustard.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [chicken, baking, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | chicken tenders
  - 1 cup | buttermilk
  - 1 tbsp | hot sauce
  - ½ cup | all-purpose flour
  - 1 tsp | garlic powder
  - 1 tsp | smoked paprika
  - To taste | salt and pepper
  - 2 | eggs, beaten
  - 1 cup | panko breadcrumbs
  - ½ cup | grated parmesan
  - Olive oil spray | for the rack
  - ¼ cup | mayonnaise
  - 2 tbsp | dijon mustard
  - 1 tbsp | honey
method:
  - Whisk the buttermilk and hot sauce together, submerge the chicken tenders, and let sit at room temperature for 15 minutes.
  - Heat the oven to 425°F and set a wire rack inside a rimmed baking sheet; coat the rack with olive oil spray.
  - Combine the flour, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper in one shallow dish, the beaten eggs in a second, and the panko and parmesan in a third.
  - Lift each tender from the buttermilk, dredge in the flour, dip in the egg, then press into the panko mixture until fully coated.
  - Arrange the tenders on the rack without touching and spray the tops lightly with oil.
  - Bake until the coating is deep golden brown and the thickest tender reaches 165°F, 18 to 20 minutes, flipping once halfway through.
  - Whisk the mayonnaise, dijon, and honey together for the dipping sauce.
---

The wire rack is what makes this work without a fryer — it lets hot air move under the chicken so the bottom crisps instead of steaming against the pan. Skip it and you get soggy-bottomed tenders no matter how good the crust looks on top.

The buttermilk soak is doing double duty: it tenderizes and it gives the flour something to cling to, so don't skip straight to the egg wash even if you're short on time. Leftovers reheat well in a 400°F oven for five minutes — the microwave will undo all of this.
