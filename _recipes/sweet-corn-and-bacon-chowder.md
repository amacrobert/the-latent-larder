---
title: Sweet Corn and Bacon Chowder
date: 2026-07-30 01:30:07 -0400
description: A cream-thickened pot built on corn cobs simmered into the stock, chunky with potato and finished with the bacon that started it.
image: /assets/images/Sweet-Corn-And-Bacon-Chowder.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 35 min
tags: [soup, pork, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 4 slices | bacon, diced
  - 1 | yellow onion, diced
  - 2 stalks | celery, diced
  - 2 tbsp | butter
  - 2 tbsp | flour
  - 3 cups | chicken stock
  - 4 cups | corn kernels, cobs reserved
  - 1 lb | Yukon gold potatoes, diced
  - 1 cup | heavy cream
  - To taste | salt and pepper
  - To finish | chives, sliced
method:
  - Render the bacon in a heavy pot over medium heat until crisp, then lift it out with a slotted spoon and set aside.
  - Add the onion and celery to the bacon fat and cook until softened, about 6 minutes.
  - Stir in the butter and flour and cook until the raw flour smell is gone, about 2 minutes.
  - Whisk in the stock, scraping up any browned bits, then drop in the reserved corn cobs and the potatoes.
  - Simmer until the potatoes are just tender when pierced, about 12 minutes, then fish out the cobs and discard them.
  - Stir in the corn kernels and cream and simmer until the corn is bright and the chowder has thickened slightly, about 8 minutes.
  - Season with salt and pepper, ladle into bowls, and top with the reserved bacon and chives.
---

The scraped cobs are doing the real work here — simmered in the stock before the kernels ever go in, they give up a corn flavor that kernels alone can't. Don't skip them, even if it means a mesh strainer for stray silk. Frozen corn works in the off-season; you'll just be simmering plain stock a little longer to make up for the missing cobs.
