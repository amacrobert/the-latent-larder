---
title: Broiled Garlic Bread with Parmesan
date: 2026-08-05 00:00:03 -0400
description: A split loaf loaded with garlic butter and finished under the broiler until the edges char — the fastest path from oven to pasta night.
image: /assets/images/Broiled-Garlic-Bread-With-Parmesan.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 8 min
tags: [bread, side, appetizer, quick, vegetarian, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 1 | French baguette or Italian loaf, halved lengthwise
  - 6 tbsp | unsalted butter, softened
  - 4 cloves | garlic, finely grated
  - 2 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped
  - ½ cup | Parmesan, finely grated
  - ½ tsp | fine salt
  - A pinch | red pepper flakes (optional)
method:
  - Position a rack 6 inches from the broiler element and set it to high.
  - Mash the butter, garlic, parsley, and salt together into an even paste.
  - Spread the garlic butter to the edges of both cut sides of the loaf, then scatter the Parmesan over the top.
  - Broil until the cheese is bubbling and the high points of the bread are charred in spots, 3 to 5 minutes — watch it the whole time, it goes from pale to burnt fast.
  - Scatter with red pepper flakes if using, slice on a diagonal, and serve hot.
---

Grate the garlic instead of mincing it — a microplane breaks it down into the butter evenly, so you don't bite into a raw, sharp chunk two inches from a piece that has none. Buy the loaf a few hours ahead if you can; bread that's slightly stale toasts crisper than one still soft from the bakery.

This is built to ride along with anything saucy — [Spaghetti Carbonara](/recipes/spaghetti-carbonara/), a bowl of [French Onion Soup](/recipes/french-onion-soup/), or straight off the sheet pan as a starter with nothing else on the table.
