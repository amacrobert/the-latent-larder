title: Mini BBQ-Glazed Meatloaf Muffins
date: 2026-08-07 18:00:03 -0400
description: Ground beef pressed into a muffin tin and glazed with barbecue sauce — a full meatloaf dinner in the time it takes rice to cook.
image: /assets/images/BBQ-Meatloaf-Muffins.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [beef, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | ground beef (85/15)
  - ¾ cup | panko breadcrumbs
  - ¼ cup | whole milk
  - 1 | large egg
  - ½ | small yellow onion, grated
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 2 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ tsp | black pepper
  - ½ cup | barbecue sauce, divided
  - 2 tbsp | brown sugar
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 400°F and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  - Stir the panko and milk together in a large bowl and let sit until the crumbs have soaked it up, about 5 minutes.
  - Add the ground beef, egg, onion, garlic, Worcestershire, salt, pepper, and ¼ cup barbecue sauce, and mix with your hands just until combined.
  - Divide the mixture evenly among the muffin cups, pressing lightly into each — pack it too tight and the loaves turn dense.
  - Whisk the remaining ¼ cup barbecue sauce with the brown sugar and spoon it over each muffin.
  - Bake until the glaze is bubbling at the edges and a thermometer inserted in the center reads 160°F, about 20 minutes.
  - Let the muffins rest in the tin for 5 minutes before lifting them out — they hold together better once they've firmed up.
---

Shaping the loaf into a muffin tin instead of one big brick is the whole trick: twelve small loaves have far more glazed surface area per bite than one, and they cook through in 20 minutes instead of the hour a full loaf needs. That makes this the version for a Tuesday, not the Sunday centerpiece.

No barbecue sauce on hand? Ketchup mixed with a splash of cider vinegar works for the glaze. The muffins freeze well after baking — thaw and reheat straight in the tin, or crumble the extras into a jar of leftover barbecue sauce for a sandwich the next day.
