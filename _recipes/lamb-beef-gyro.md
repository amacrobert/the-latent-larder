---
title: Lamb and Beef Gyro with Tzatziki
date: 2026-08-12 12:00:03 -0400
description: Ground lamb and beef processed to a fine paste, baked as a loaf, then sliced thin and crisped in a skillet the way a spit would — no vertical rotisserie required.
image: /assets/images/Lamb-Beef-Gyro.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 25 min
cook_time: 1 hr 15 min
tags: [lamb, beef, sandwich, main]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | ground lamb
  - 1 lb | ground beef
  - 1 small | onion, quartered
  - 4 cloves | garlic
  - 1 tbsp | dried oregano
  - 2 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | ground coriander
  - 1 tsp | smoked paprika
  - 1½ tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ tsp | black pepper
  - 1 cup | plain Greek yogurt
  - ½ | cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1 tbsp | lemon juice
  - 4 | pitas, warmed
  - 1 | tomato, thinly sliced
  - ½ | red onion, thinly sliced
  - To finish | chopped dill
method:
  - Pulse the onion and 4 cloves garlic in a food processor until they break down to a coarse paste.
  - Add the lamb, beef, oregano, cumin, coriander, paprika, salt, and pepper, and pulse in short bursts until the mixture turns pale, tacky, and holds together when pinched — this is what lets it slice instead of crumble.
  - Pack the mixture tightly into a loaf pan lined with plastic wrap, pressing out any air pockets, then set the loaf pan inside a larger roasting pan and add hot water halfway up its sides.
  - Bake at 325°F until the center reaches 160°F on an instant-read thermometer, about 1 hour.
  - Let the loaf rest 15 minutes, then unmold and slice as thin as you can manage.
  - Whisk the yogurt, grated cucumber, minced garlic, olive oil, and lemon juice with a pinch of salt for the tzatziki, and chill until needed.
  - Crisp the sliced meat in a hot, lightly oiled skillet in batches until the edges catch and char, a minute or two per side.
  - Pile the crisped meat into warm pitas with tomato, red onion, dill, and a generous spoonful of tzatziki.
---

The food processor step is the whole trick — home versions that skip it and just pan-fry crumbled ground meat end up with taco filling, not gyro. Pulsing the meat into a bonded paste before it bakes is what lets it hold a slice thin enough to crisp at the edges. The water bath keeps a lean lamb-beef mix from drying out over the hour it takes to cook through; don't skip it even though the loaf itself never touches the water.

Make the loaf up to two days ahead and only slice and crisp what you need — cold leftover loaf slices just as cleanly and crisps just as well straight from the fridge.
