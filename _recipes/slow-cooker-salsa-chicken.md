---
title: Slow Cooker Shredded Salsa Chicken
date: 2026-07-31 16:30:03 -0400
description: Chicken breasts braised low and slow in salsa until they shred themselves — dump it in before work, pull it apart for dinner.
image: /assets/images/Slow-Cooker-Shredded-Salsa-Chicken.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 6 hrs
tags: [chicken, slow, main, make-ahead, spicy]
ingredients:
  - 2 lb | boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  - 1½ cups | salsa, jarred or fresh
  - 1 | small onion, sliced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp | chili powder
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp | kosher salt
  - 1 | lime, juiced
  - To finish | chopped cilantro
method:
  - Stir the salsa, onion, garlic, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and salt together in the slow cooker.
  - Nestle the chicken breasts into the sauce and spoon some over the top so they're mostly submerged.
  - Cover and cook on low until the chicken shreds apart with no resistance, 6 to 7 hours (or high for 3 to 4 hours).
  - Transfer the chicken to a cutting board and shred with two forks.
  - Return the shredded chicken to the slow cooker and stir until every piece is coated in sauce.
  - Stir in the lime juice and taste for salt.
  - Serve warm, topped with cilantro.
---

The salsa is most of the flavor here, so use one you'd eat with a chip — a thin, watery jarred salsa makes a thin, watery sauce. If it looks loose after shredding, tip the lid off and cook on high for 10 minutes to tighten it up.

This is built to become something else: tacos, burrito bowls, nachos, quesadillas, a baked potato topper. It keeps four days in the fridge and freezes well in its sauce, so it's worth doubling.
