---
title: Salisbury Steak with Mushroom Onion Gravy
date: 2026-08-04 09:00:03 -0400
description: Seared ground beef patties simmered in a deep brown mushroom-onion gravy, built entirely in one skillet.
image: /assets/images/Salisbury-Steak.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [beef, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | ground beef (85/15)
  - ½ cup | breadcrumbs
  - 1 | egg
  - 2 tbsp | ketchup
  - 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce, divided
  - 1 tsp | garlic powder
  - To taste | salt and pepper
  - 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
  - 4 tbsp | unsalted butter, divided
  - 1 | large onion, thinly sliced
  - 8 oz | cremini mushrooms, sliced
  - 3 tbsp | all-purpose flour
  - 2½ cups | beef stock
  - 1 tsp | Dijon mustard
method:
  - Mix the ground beef, breadcrumbs, egg, ketchup, 1 tsp of the Worcestershire, garlic powder, salt, and pepper by hand until just combined.
  - Shape into 4 oval patties about ¾ inch thick.
  - Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high and sear the patties until deeply browned on both sides but not cooked through, about 3 minutes per side; transfer to a plate.
  - Melt 2 tbsp of the butter in the same skillet and cook the onion, stirring occasionally, until soft and golden, about 8 minutes.
  - Add the mushrooms and remaining 2 tbsp butter and cook until the mushrooms have released their liquid and browned, about 6 minutes.
  - Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir until no dry patches remain, about 1 minute.
  - Whisk in the beef stock a little at a time, then stir in the remaining Worcestershire and the Dijon, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
  - Bring to a simmer and cook until the gravy has thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 5 minutes.
  - Nestle the patties back into the gravy and simmer until cooked through, about 8 minutes.
---

The patties go in raw-in-the-middle on purpose — searing them fully at this stage just dries them out before they finish cooking in the gravy. Shape them oval, not round; a Salisbury steak isn't a burger, and the flatter shape sears more crust per patty.

No cremini on hand, white button mushrooms work fine — the color and depth of the gravy come from the fond and the flour, not the mushroom variety. Serve over mashed potatoes so the gravy has somewhere to go.
