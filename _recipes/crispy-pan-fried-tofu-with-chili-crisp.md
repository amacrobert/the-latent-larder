---
title: Crispy Pan-Fried Tofu with Chili Crisp
date: 2026-07-26 11:45:22 -0400
description: Tofu fried hard in a cast-iron pan until the crust shatters, then tossed in chili crisp — a fifteen-minute main that doesn't miss the meat.
image: /assets/images/Crispy-Pan-Fried-Tofu-With-Chili-Crisp.webp
servings: 2
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [tofu, quick, vegetarian, main, spicy]
ingredients:
  - 1 block | firm tofu, pressed
  - 3 tbsp | cornstarch
  - 3 tbsp | neutral oil
  - ¼ cup | chili crisp
  - 2 | scallions, sliced
  - 1 tbsp | soy sauce
  - 1 tsp | rice vinegar
  - To finish | sesame seeds
method:
  - Press the tofu block between towels under a weight for at least 15 minutes, until it stops leaking water.
  - Cut the tofu into 1-inch cubes.
  - Toss the cubes in cornstarch until every side is coated and no dry patches remain.
  - Heat the oil in a cast-iron or nonstick skillet over high heat until it shimmers.
  - Add the tofu in a single layer and fry undisturbed until deeply golden on one side, then flip each piece and repeat until all sides are crisp.
  - Off the heat, add the chili crisp, soy sauce, and rice vinegar, tossing to coat while the crust is still hot.
  - Scatter scallions and sesame seeds over the top and serve immediately.
---

The whole dish hinges on the press: skip it and the tofu weeps water into the hot oil, the crust never sets, and you get sad, oily cubes instead of a shattering one. Cornstarch matters too — flour goes gummy where cornstarch goes glassy.

No chili crisp on hand, thin a spoonful of gochujang with the rice vinegar instead. Serve it over rice to catch the sauce, or fold it into lettuce cups for something lighter.
