---
title: Spinach and Feta Stuffed Chicken Breasts
date: 2026-08-10 18:00:02 -0400
description: Butterflied breasts packed with a garlicky spinach-feta filling, seared for a crust, then finished in the oven so the middle stays juicy.
image: /assets/images/Stuffed-Chicken-Breasts.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [chicken, main, quick, high-protein]
ingredients:
  - 4 (6 oz) | chicken breasts, boneless, skinless
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil, plus more for searing
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 5 oz | baby spinach
  - 4 oz | feta, crumbled
  - 2 oz | cream cheese, softened
  - ¼ tsp | red pepper flakes
  - To taste | kosher salt and black pepper
  - Toothpicks | for securing
method:
  - Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium and cook the garlic until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Add the spinach in handfuls, stirring until fully wilted, 2 to 3 minutes. Tip into a colander and press out as much liquid as you can, then let cool.
  - Stir the cooled spinach into the feta, cream cheese, and red pepper flakes until evenly combined.
  - Lay a chicken breast flat and slice horizontally through the thickest part, stopping about ½ inch from the far edge so it opens like a book. Repeat with the rest.
  - Season the inside and outside of each breast with salt and pepper, then divide the filling among them and fold closed. Secure the opening with a toothpick or two.
  - Heat a thin layer of olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Sear the chicken until deeply golden, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
  - Transfer the skillet to a 400°F oven and bake until the chicken reaches 165°F at the thickest part, 12 to 15 minutes.
  - Rest 5 minutes before pulling the toothpicks and slicing.
---

Pressing the water out of the cooked spinach is the step that keeps the filling from turning the chicken soggy from the inside — skip it and the extra liquid leaks out the seam and steams the crust you just worked for. Stop slicing the pocket well short of the far edge, too; punch through and the filling has nowhere to stay put.

Swap the feta for shredded mozzarella and add a spoonful of sun-dried tomato if you want it milder and richer instead of tangy. Leftovers slice cold into a strong lunch salad the next day.
