---
title: Pan-Fried Pork and Napa Cabbage Dumplings
date: 2026-07-26
description: Seared crisp on one side and steamed soft on the other, gone before the second batch is out of the pan.
image: /assets/images/Pan-Fried-Pork-And-Napa-Cabbage-Dumplings.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 45 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [pork, appetizer, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | ground pork
  - 2 cups | napa cabbage, finely chopped
  - 3 | scallions, minced
  - 1 tbsp | fresh ginger, grated
  - 2 tbsp | soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp | sesame oil
  - 1 tsp | Kosher salt
  - 1 tsp | cornstarch
  - 30 | dumpling wrappers
  - 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
  - To finish | rice vinegar, soy sauce, and chili oil, for dipping
method:
  - Toss the chopped cabbage with the salt and let it sit for 10 minutes, then squeeze it by the handful until it stops releasing water.
  - Mix the cabbage with the pork, scallions, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, and cornstarch until the mixture holds together when pressed.
  - Spoon a scant tablespoon of filling into the center of each wrapper, wet the edges with water, and pleat closed into a tight half-moon.
  - Heat the vegetable oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high and arrange the dumplings flat-side down in a single layer.
  - Fry undisturbed until the bottoms are deep golden brown, about 3 minutes.
  - Add ¼ cup water, cover immediately, and steam until the water has evaporated and the wrappers turn translucent, about 5 minutes.
  - Uncover and let the bottoms recrisp for a minute before sliding the dumplings onto a plate, seared side up.
---

The squeeze is the part people skip and the part that matters — cabbage left wet turns the filling to soup and splits the wrappers in the pan. Wring it out harder than feels necessary; it should look almost dry before it meets the pork.

Freeze uncooked dumplings on a tray until solid, then bag them and fry straight from frozen, adding a minute or two to the steaming step. Ground chicken works in place of pork, and a mix of minced mushrooms and crumbled firm tofu makes a fair vegetarian version.
