---
title: Oven-Toasted Chex Mix with Garlic and Worcestershire
date: 2026-08-07 09:00:03 -0400
description: Corn and rice cereal, pretzels, and nuts tossed in garlicky Worcestershire butter and baked low and slow until every piece is crisp through.
image: /assets/images/Oven-Toasted-Chex-Mix.webp
servings: 12
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 1 hour
tags: [snack, appetizer, party, vegetarian, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 3 cups | corn Chex
  - 3 cups | rice Chex
  - 1 cup | mini pretzel twists
  - 1 cup | mixed nuts
  - 6 tbsp | unsalted butter
  - 2 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce
  - 1½ tsp | garlic powder
  - 1½ tsp | onion powder
  - ¾ tsp | seasoned salt
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 250°F and set a rack in the middle position.
  - Melt the butter in a small saucepan, then whisk in the Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, and seasoned salt until smooth.
  - Spread the Chex, pretzels, and nuts in a large roasting pan or two rimmed sheet pans.
  - Pour the butter mixture over the top and fold with a wide spatula until every piece is coated and no dry cereal sits at the bottom.
  - Bake, stirring every 15 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp all the way through, about 1 hour.
  - Spread onto paper towels or a clean sheet pan to cool completely — it firms up further as it cools, so don't judge crispness straight from the oven.
---

The low oven is the whole technique. Anything hotter browns the outside of the cereal before the butter finishes soaking in, so you end up with a mix that's toasty on the surface and soft underneath. Two hundred fifty degrees and an hour, with a stir every 15 minutes, gets the seasoning all the way into the piece instead of sitting on top of it.

Swap the mixed nuts for cashews or peanuts, or add a cup of bagel chips or cheese crackers along with the pretzels — the ratio of cereal to butter is what matters, not the exact mix-ins. It keeps in an airtight container at room temperature for a couple of weeks, which makes it one of the few party snacks actually worth making a full recipe of ahead.
