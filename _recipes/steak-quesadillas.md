---
title: Skillet Steak Quesadillas with Pepper Jack
date: 2026-08-01 02:30:03 -0400
description: Seared skirt steak and melted pepper jack folded into a charred flour tortilla, ready before the rice even finishes.
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [beef, quick, main, cheese, spicy]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | skirt steak
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | chili powder
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
  - 1 | green bell pepper, thinly sliced
  - ½ | red onion, thinly sliced
  - 8 | flour tortillas (8-inch)
  - 2 cups | shredded pepper jack cheese
  - ¼ cup | chopped cilantro
  - To finish | lime wedges and sour cream
method:
  - Season the steak all over with cumin, chili powder, and salt.
  - Heat the oil in a large skillet over high heat until shimmering, then sear the steak 3-4 minutes per side until deeply browned and a thermometer reads 130°F for medium-rare.
  - Rest the steak 10 minutes, then slice thin against the grain.
  - Wipe out the skillet, add the bell pepper and onion, and cook over medium-high heat until softened and charred at the edges, about 5 minutes.
  - Lay 4 tortillas flat, scatter cheese, the sliced steak, and the pepper-onion mixture over half of each, then fold to close.
  - Wipe the skillet clean, set over medium heat, and cook each quesadilla 2-3 minutes per side until the tortilla is crisp and golden and the cheese has fully melted.
  - Cut into wedges and scatter with cilantro.
---

The steak wants a screaming-hot pan and a real rest — slice it too soon and the juices puddle in the tortilla instead of staying in the meat. If the crust looks pale after four minutes, the pan wasn't hot enough; let it heat longer next time rather than cooking the steak longer.

Leftover grilled steak or a rotisserie chicken both work in place of the seared skirt steak, no other changes needed. Serve straight from the skillet with lime wedges and sour cream — nobody is waiting for you to plate this.
