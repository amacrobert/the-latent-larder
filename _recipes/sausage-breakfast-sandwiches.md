description: The fast-food classic built at home — a well-browned sausage patty and a crispy-edged fried egg stacked with melted cheddar between a toasted English muffin.
image: /assets/images/Sausage-Breakfast-Sandwiches.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [breakfast, quick, sandwich, eggs, pork]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | bulk breakfast sausage
  - 4 | English muffins, split
  - 4 | large eggs
  - 4 slices | sharp cheddar
  - 1 tbsp | butter
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
  - To finish | hot sauce
method:
  - Divide the sausage into 4 patties a bit wider than the English muffins — they'll shrink and puff as they cook.
  - Cook the patties in a dry skillet over medium heat until deeply browned and cooked through, about 4 minutes per side; transfer to a plate.
  - Toast the muffin halves cut-side down in the rendered sausage fat until golden.
  - Melt the butter in the same skillet, crack in the eggs, and season with salt and pepper; cook undisturbed until the edges turn brown and lacy and the whites are just set, about 2 minutes.
  - Lay a slice of cheddar on each egg and cover the pan for 30 seconds, until it slumps.
  - Stack muffin bottom, sausage patty, cheese-topped egg, and muffin top, and serve hot.
---

The egg is the one part worth doing deliberately: cook it hot and mostly still, so the edges crisp and brown while the center stays soft. A gently cooked, uniformly pale egg is what separates a diner sandwich from this one.

These hold well wrapped in foil for a grab-and-go morning — assemble, wrap while hot, and they'll steam themselves soft over 10 minutes if you're not eating right away. Swap in Canadian bacon or a couple strips of crisped regular bacon for the sausage patty if that's what's in the fridge.
