---
title: Chocolate Peanut Butter Puppy Chow
date: 2026-08-09 00:00:02 -0400
description: Corn Chex tossed in melted chocolate and peanut butter, then shaken in powdered sugar until every piece turns matte white — no oven, ready in twenty minutes.
image: /assets/images/Chocolate-Peanut-Butter-Puppy-Chow.webp
servings: 12
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 5 min
tags: [snack, dessert, party, no-bake, vegetarian, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 9 cups | corn Chex cereal
  - 1 cup | semisweet chocolate chips
  - ½ cup | creamy peanut butter
  - ¼ cup | unsalted butter
  - 1 tsp | vanilla extract
  - 1½ cups | powdered sugar, plus more for dusting
method:
  - Measure the Chex into your largest mixing bowl.
  - Melt the chocolate chips, peanut butter, and butter together in a microwave-safe bowl in 30-second bursts, stirring between each, until smooth and glossy.
  - Stir the vanilla into the melted chocolate mixture.
  - Pour the chocolate mixture over the Chex and fold gently with a rubber spatula until every piece is coated and no dry cereal remains at the bottom — go slow so the squares don't crumble.
  - Sift the powdered sugar over the coated cereal in two batches, tossing in a large paper bag or bowl with a lid between additions until every piece is dusted matte white with no wet or dark patches showing.
  - Spread onto a parchment-lined sheet pan in a single layer and let sit until the coating is set and no longer tacky to the touch, about 15 minutes.
---

The chocolate has to be barely melted, not hot, or it seizes the moment the powdered sugar hits it — thirty-second microwave bursts with a stir between each get you there without scorching. Shaking the sugar on in two batches inside a paper bag beats a bowl and spoon; every piece gets coated instead of just the top layer.

Swap in pretzel sticks for a third of the Chex if you want a salty crunch running through it, or crushed peanut butter cups pressed on top while the coating is still tacky for a party-tray version.
