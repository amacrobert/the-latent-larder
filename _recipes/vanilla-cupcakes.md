---
title: Vanilla Cupcakes with Chocolate Buttercream
date: 2026-08-03 04:00:02 -0400
description: A basic creamed-butter cupcake, baked in liners and piped with fudgy chocolate buttercream once it's fully cool.
image: /assets/images/Vanilla-Cupcakes.webp
servings: 12
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 18 min
tags: [dessert, baking, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1½ cups | all-purpose flour
  - 1½ tsp | baking powder
  - ¼ tsp | salt
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter, softened
  - 1 cup | granulated sugar
  - 2 | large eggs, room temperature
  - 1 tsp | vanilla extract
  - ½ cup | whole milk, room temperature
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter, softened, for buttercream
  - 2 cups | powdered sugar
  - ¼ cup | cocoa powder
  - 3 tbsp | heavy cream
  - ½ tsp | vanilla extract, for buttercream
  - A pinch | salt, for buttercream
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 350°F and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
  - Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl.
  - Cream the butter and sugar together until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, scraping the bowl between additions.
  - Add the dry ingredients in three additions, alternating with the milk, mixing just until no streaks of flour remain.
  - Divide the batter evenly among the liners, filling each about two-thirds full.
  - Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean, 16 to 18 minutes.
  - Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack and cool completely before frosting.
  - Beat the butter for the buttercream until smooth, then add the powdered sugar and cocoa powder alternately with the cream, beating until light and spreadable.
  - Pipe or spread the buttercream over the cooled cupcakes.
---

Room-temperature butter, eggs, and milk are what keep this crumb even — cold ingredients seize the batter and you end up with dense pockets around the edges. Stop mixing the moment the flour disappears; a cupcake batter worked past that point domes unevenly and turns tough instead of tender.

Frost only once the cupcakes are fully cool, or the buttercream slides right off. For a plain vanilla version, drop the cocoa powder and add an extra tablespoon of cream to keep the texture the same.
