---
title: Butterscotch Blondies with Toasted Pecans
date: 2026-08-03 07:00:03 -0400
description: A dense, chewy bar built on melted butter and brown sugar instead of chocolate, cut small because the edges are the good part.
image: /assets/images/Butterscotch-Blondies-With-Toasted-Pecans.webp
servings: 16 bars
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 30 min
tags: [dessert, baking, quick]
ingredients:
  - 1 cup | pecans, chopped
  - 1 cup | unsalted butter, melted
  - 2 cups | packed brown sugar
  - 2 | large eggs
  - 1 | egg yolk
  - 2 tsp | vanilla extract
  - 2 cups | all-purpose flour
  - 1 tsp | baking powder
  - ½ tsp | fine salt
  - To finish | flaky salt
method:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and line a 9x13-inch pan with parchment, leaving an overhang on two sides.
  - Toast the pecans on a baking sheet in the oven until fragrant, about 6 minutes, then set aside to cool.
  - Whisk the melted butter and brown sugar together until glossy and no lumps of sugar remain.
  - Whisk in the eggs, yolk, and vanilla until the batter thickens slightly and turns a shade lighter.
  - Fold in the flour, baking powder, and salt just until no dry streaks remain, then fold in the toasted pecans.
  - Spread the batter into the pan, scatter with flaky salt, and bake until the edges have pulled from the sides and a toothpick from the center comes out with a few moist crumbs, 26 to 30 minutes.
  - Cool completely in the pan before lifting out by the parchment and cutting into squares.
---

The butterscotch flavor here comes entirely from browning the sugar against melted butter, not from butterscotch chips — and the melted butter (rather than creamed) is what keeps these dense and chewy instead of cakey. Pull the pan while the center still looks a little underset; it firms up as it cools, and a fully clean toothpick means you've gone past chewy into dry.

Swap the pecans for walnuts or a cup of chocolate chips without changing anything else. They keep well wrapped at room temperature for four days, and freeze cut into squares for longer.
