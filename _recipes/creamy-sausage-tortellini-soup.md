---
title: Creamy Sausage Tortellini Soup with Spinach
date: 2026-08-09 15:00:03 -0400
description: Browned Italian sausage, cheese tortellini, and wilted spinach in a tomato-cream broth — a 30-minute pot that eats like a casserole.
image: /assets/images/Creamy-Sausage-Tortellini-Soup.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [soup, pork, quick, main, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | Italian sausage, casings removed
  - 1 | yellow onion, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 4 cups | chicken stock
  - 1 can (14 oz) | crushed tomatoes
  - 1 tsp | dried oregano
  - ½ tsp | red pepper flakes
  - 9 oz | fresh cheese tortellini
  - 1 cup | heavy cream
  - 3 cups | baby spinach
  - ½ cup | Parmesan, grated, plus more to finish
  - Salt and pepper, to taste
method:
  - Brown the sausage in a soup pot over medium-high heat, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink remains, about 6 minutes.
  - Add the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 4 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Pour in the stock and crushed tomatoes, then stir in the oregano and red pepper flakes. Bring to a boil.
  - Add the tortellini and cook until they float and are tender, 3 to 4 minutes.
  - Stir in the cream and Parmesan and return to a bare simmer.
  - Add the spinach a handful at a time, stirring until each addition wilts down.
  - Season with salt and pepper and serve with more Parmesan over the top.
---

Fresh tortellini, not dried — it cooks straight in the broth in a few minutes and holds its shape, where a dried shell would need to be boiled separately or it soaks up the whole pot. If all you can find is frozen, drop it in straight from the freezer and give it another minute or two.

This one goes from raw sausage to the table in well under an hour and reheats fine the next day, though the tortellini keep swelling a little in the fridge — thin leftovers with a splash of stock when you warm them back up.
