---
title: Lemon-Parmesan Orzo with Wilted Spinach
date: 2026-08-07 03:00:02 -0400
description: Orzo toasted in butter until nutty, then simmered pilaf-style in broth with lemon and Parmesan — a fast side for whatever's on the other burner.
image: /assets/images/Lemon-Parmesan-Orzo.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [pasta, side, quick, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp | unsalted butter
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1½ cups | orzo
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 2¼ cups | vegetable broth
  - 3 cups | baby spinach
  - 1 | lemon, zested and juiced
  - ½ cup | grated Parmesan
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
method:
  - Melt the butter with the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  - Add the orzo and toast, stirring often, until golden brown and smelling nutty, 3 to 4 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Pour in the broth, bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until the orzo is tender and the liquid is absorbed, about 10 minutes.
  - Stir in the spinach and cook, uncovered, until just wilted, about 1 minute.
  - Off the heat, stir in the lemon zest, lemon juice, and Parmesan. Season with salt and pepper.
---

Toast the orzo dry in the fat before any liquid goes in — that's what gets you the nutty flavor instead of just soft starch. Skip it and the dish tastes like plain boiled pasta no matter how much lemon and Parmesan you add at the end.

Swap the spinach for a cup of frozen peas stirred in with the cheese, no cooking needed beyond thawing. This sits well next to anything you'd normally serve over rice — a simple seared chicken breast or pan-fried fish both work.
