---
title: Instant Pot Mississippi Pot Roast
date: 2026-08-04 13:00:04 -0400
description: Chuck roast pressure-cooked with pepperoncini, ranch seasoning, and a whole stick of butter until it shreds into a tangy, buttery gravy.
image: /assets/images/Instant-Pot-Mississippi-Pot-Roast.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 65 min
tags: [beef, main, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 3 lb | beef chuck roast
  - To taste | kosher salt and black pepper
  - 2 tbsp | vegetable oil
  - 1 oz | ranch seasoning mix (1 packet)
  - 1 oz | au jus gravy mix (1 packet)
  - ½ cup | beef broth
  - 8 | pepperoncini, plus ¼ cup of their brine
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter (1 stick)
method:
  - Season the chuck roast all over with salt and pepper.
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté, heat the oil, and sear the roast until deeply browned, about 4 minutes per side, then turn the pot off.
  - Sprinkle the ranch seasoning and au jus mix over the roast, then pour in the broth and pepperoncini brine.
  - Scatter the pepperoncini around the roast and set the butter on top whole.
  - Seal the lid and cook on high pressure for 55 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes before opening.
  - Lift the roast onto a platter and shred it with two forks, discarding any large pieces of fat.
  - Stir the shredded meat back into the buttery pot liquid and serve spooned over mashed potatoes with the pepperoncini scattered on top.
---

The ranch packet and au jus mix are doing the seasoning work a dozen individual spices would otherwise do, and the butter is not optional — it melts into the pot liquid over the pressure-cooking time and turns into the gravy, not just a garnish on top.

Chuck breaks down fast under pressure, so this comes together in about an hour instead of the eight a slow cooker version needs. Leftovers make a very good sandwich piled onto a toasted roll with a few extra pepperoncini.
