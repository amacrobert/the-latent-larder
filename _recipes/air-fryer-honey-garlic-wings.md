---
title: Air Fryer Honey Garlic Chicken Wings
date: 2026-08-06 12:00:03 -0400
description: No oil bath and no oven preheat — just a hot basket and a sticky garlic glaze that clings instead of pooling.
image: /assets/images/Air-Fryer-Honey-Garlic-Wings.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [chicken, appetizer, quick]
ingredients:
  - 2½ lb | chicken wings, split at the joint
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ tsp | black pepper
  - 4 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 5 tbsp | honey
  - 3 tbsp | soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp | rice vinegar
  - 1 tsp | toasted sesame oil
  - 1 tsp | cornstarch
  - To finish | sliced scallions and sesame seeds
method:
  - Pat the wings dry, then toss with the salt and pepper.
  - Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket, without crowding, and cook at 380°F for 12 minutes.
  - Flip and cook until deep golden and crisp at the edges, another 10 to 12 minutes.
  - While the wings finish, combine the garlic, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, and cornstarch in a small saucepan and simmer, whisking, until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Toss the hot wings in the glaze until fully coated and serve right away with scallions and sesame seeds.
---

The cornstarch is what keeps this from turning into a syrupy puddle at the bottom of the bowl — without it the honey just runs off the skin. Simmer the glaze only until it coats a spoon; much past that and it seizes into taffy when it hits the cool wings.

Work in two batches if your basket holds less than a single layer. Crowded wings steam instead of crisping, and there's no rack trick here to save them the way there is in the oven.
