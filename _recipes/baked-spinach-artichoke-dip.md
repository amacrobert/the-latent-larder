title: Baked Spinach Artichoke Dip with Parmesan

description: A hot, cheese-pulled dip built for a crowd around a bowl of chips, done in one baking dish start to finish.
image: /assets/images/Baked-Spinach-Artichoke-Dip-With-Parmesan.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [appetizer, dip, vegetarian, baking, cheese, quick]
ingredients:
  - 8 oz | cream cheese, softened
  - ½ cup | sour cream
  - ¼ cup | mayonnaise
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 10 oz | frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  - 14 oz | canned artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  - 1 cup | shredded mozzarella
  - ¾ cup | grated Parmesan, divided
  - ½ tsp | red pepper flakes
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
  - To finish | sliced baguette or tortilla chips
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 375°F and butter a small baking dish.
  - Beat the cream cheese, sour cream, and mayonnaise together until smooth.
  - Stir in the garlic, spinach, artichokes, mozzarella, and half the Parmesan until evenly combined.
  - Season with red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper.
  - Scrape into the baking dish, smooth the top, and scatter the remaining Parmesan over it.
  - Bake until the top is deeply golden and bubbling at the edges, 20 to 25 minutes.
  - Let it sit five minutes before serving hot with bread or chips.
---

Squeeze the spinach dry like you mean it — a fistful at a time, twisted in a clean towel until no more water runs out. Skip this and the dip separates into a greasy, watery mess under the cheese instead of holding together.

It reheats better than most party food: assemble the whole thing a day ahead, cover, and refrigerate unbaked, then add five minutes to the bake time straight from the fridge.
