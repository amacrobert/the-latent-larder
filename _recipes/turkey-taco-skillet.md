---
title: One-Pan Turkey Taco Skillet
date: 2026-08-01 00:00:03 -0400
description: Ground turkey, rice, and black beans simmered in taco spices and finished with melted cheddar — one skillet, no sides required.
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [turkey, rice, quick, main, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1 lb | ground turkey
  - 1 | onion, diced
  - 1 | bell pepper, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp | chili powder
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | smoked paprika
  - 1 cup | long-grain white rice
  - 1 can | diced tomatoes, with juices (14.5 oz)
  - 1¾ cups | chicken broth
  - 1 can | black beans, drained and rinsed (15 oz)
  - 1 cup | shredded cheddar
  - To taste | kosher salt
  - To finish | lime wedges, cilantro, and sliced scallions
method:
  - Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high and brown the turkey, breaking it into crumbles, until no pink remains, about 6 minutes.
  - Push the turkey to the side, add the onion and bell pepper, and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  - Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cumin, and smoked paprika and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Add the rice, diced tomatoes, and chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
  - Bring to a simmer, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed, about 18 minutes.
  - Stir in the black beans and season with salt.
  - Scatter the cheese over the top, cover, and let it sit off the heat until melted, about 2 minutes.
  - Finish with lime, cilantro, and scallions before serving.
---

Keep the lid on while the rice simmers — every peek lets out steam the rice needs to cook through, and lifting it more than once is the difference between tender grains and a crunchy center. If the liquid's gone before the rice is done, splash in a little more broth rather than cranking the heat.

Ground chicken works as a straight swap for the turkey. For a lighter version, cut the rice to ½ cup and add a drained can of corn along with the beans — it stretches the skillet without drying it out.
