---
title: Lemon Bars with Shortbread Crust
date: 2026-08-01 00:30:03 -0400
description: A blind-baked shortbread base under a puckery custard, cut into squares that hold their shape at room temperature.
image: /assets/images/Lemon-Bars-With-Shortbread-Crust.webp
servings: 16 bars
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 40 min
tags: [dessert, baking, fruit, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1½ cups | all-purpose flour
  - ½ cup | powdered sugar, plus more for dusting
  - ¼ tsp | salt
  - ¾ cup | unsalted butter, cold, cubed
  - 4 | large eggs
  - 1½ cups | granulated sugar
  - 6 tbsp | all-purpose flour
  - ¾ cup | fresh lemon juice
  - 2 tbsp | lemon zest
  - Pinch | salt
method:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and line a 9x13-inch pan with parchment, leaving an overhang on two sides.
  - Pulse the flour, powdered sugar, and salt in a food processor, then add the butter and pulse until the mixture looks like coarse sand.
  - Press the crumbs firmly and evenly into the bottom of the pan and bake until the edges turn pale gold, about 18 minutes.
  - While the crust bakes, whisk the eggs, granulated sugar, flour, lemon juice, zest, and salt until smooth.
  - Pour the filling over the hot crust and return to the oven until the center is set and no longer jiggles when the pan is nudged, 18 to 22 minutes.
  - Cool completely on a rack, then chill at least 2 hours before lifting out by the parchment and cutting into squares.
  - Dust with powdered sugar just before serving.
---

The flour in the filling is doing real work — skip it and the bars weep on the plate instead of holding a clean edge when cut. Bake the crust until it's actually gone pale gold, not just set; a pale, underbaked base turns soggy under the wet filling within a day.

Chill the bars fully before cutting, and wipe the knife between cuts for squares instead of streaks. A few tablespoons of the lemon juice can be swapped for lime if you want something sharper.
