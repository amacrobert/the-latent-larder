---
title: Crispy Baked Buffalo Wings
date: 2026-07-31 10:00:04 -0400
description: Baking powder and a wire rack get the skin shatter-crisp with no fryer, then a hot sauce-butter toss right before they hit the table.
image: /assets/images/Baked-Buffalo-Wings.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 45 min
tags: [chicken, appetizer, spicy, baking]
ingredients:
  - 3 lb | chicken wings, split at the joint
  - 1 tbsp | baking powder
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ tsp | black pepper
  - 6 tbsp | unsalted butter
  - ½ cup | hot sauce (such as Frank's)
  - 1 tbsp | honey
  - To finish | celery sticks and blue cheese dressing
method:
  - Pat the wings completely dry, then toss in a bowl with the baking powder, salt, and pepper until every piece is evenly coated.
  - Arrange skin side up in a single layer on a wire rack set over a baking sheet, with space between pieces.
  - Bake at 425°F, flipping once, until the skin is deep golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped, about 45 minutes.
  - While the wings bake, melt the butter with the hot sauce and honey in a small saucepan, whisking until glossy and combined.
  - Toss the hot wings in the sauce until fully coated and serve immediately with celery and blue cheese dressing.
---

The baking powder is doing the work a deep fryer usually does: it raises the surface pH so the skin browns faster and pulls moisture out as it bakes, which is the difference between crisp skin and rubbery skin at 425°F. Don't skip patting the wings dry first, or the powder clumps instead of coating evenly.

Toss the wings in sauce right before serving, not before they finish baking — sauced skin sitting under residual heat goes soft in minutes. If you want a smokier version, swap half the hot sauce for a smoked chili sauce, but keep the butter ratio the same so it still clings.
