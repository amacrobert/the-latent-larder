---
title: Crispy Pan-Fried Chicken Parmesan
date: 2026-07-31 12:30:03 -0400
description: Breaded cutlets fried until the crust sets, then sauced only down the center so the edges stay crackling under the broiler.
image: /assets/images/Pan-Fried-Chicken-Parmesan.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [chicken, cheese, baking, main, quick]
ingredients:
  - 4 | chicken breasts, halved horizontally into cutlets
  - Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  - 1 cup | all-purpose flour
  - 2 | eggs, beaten
  - 1½ cups | panko breadcrumbs
  - ½ cup | grated Parmesan, plus more to finish
  - 3 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1½ cups | marinara sauce
  - 8 oz | fresh mozzarella, sliced
  - To finish | torn basil
method:
  - Pound each cutlet between plastic wrap to an even ½ inch, then season both sides with salt and pepper.
  - Set up three shallow dishes: flour, beaten eggs, and panko mixed with the Parmesan.
  - Dredge each cutlet in flour, tapping off the excess, then egg, then press hard into the panko so the crumbs hold.
  - Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until it shimmers.
  - Fry the cutlets in batches, 3 to 4 minutes a side, until deep golden and the crust feels set when pressed.
  - Move the cutlets to a baking sheet, spoon marinara over just the center of each, leaving a bare border of crust, then top with mozzarella.
  - Broil until the cheese is blistered and bubbling, 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Scatter with basil and extra Parmesan before serving.
---

The sauce goes down the middle only, never edge to edge — cover the whole cutlet and the crust you just fried turns to paste under the broiler. A bare rim of crumbs stays crackling long enough to get the plate to the table.

Pound the cutlets to one thickness before you bread them; a thick end left unpounded will still be pink when the thin end is dry. Good over spaghetti tossed with a little extra marinara, or on its own with nothing but the basil.
