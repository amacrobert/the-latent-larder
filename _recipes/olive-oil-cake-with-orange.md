---
title: Olive Oil Cake with Orange
date: 2026-07-26
description: A dense, moist cake that keeps for days and tastes better on the second.
image: /assets/images/Olive-Oil-Cake-With-Orange.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 45 min
tags: [dessert, baking, fruit]
ingredients:
  - 1¾ cups | flour
  - 1 tsp | baking powder
  - ½ tsp | baking soda
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - 1 cup | olive oil
  - 1½ cups | granulated sugar
  - 3 | eggs
  - 2 | oranges, zested
  - ¼ cup | orange juice
  - ¼ cup | plain yogurt
method:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9-inch round cake pan, lining the bottom with parchment.
  - Whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  - In a separate bowl, whisk the olive oil and sugar until the mixture turns pale and slightly thickened, about 2 minutes.
  - Whisk in the eggs one at a time, then the orange zest, orange juice, and yogurt.
  - Fold the dry ingredients into the wet in two additions, stopping as soon as no streaks of flour remain.
  - Pour into the prepared pan and bake until a skewer comes out clean and the top springs back when pressed, 40 to 45 minutes.
  - Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack to cool completely before slicing.
---

Olive oil does two jobs a butter cake can't: it keeps the crumb moist for
days without drying out, and it carries its own flavor instead of hiding
behind vanilla, so pick an oil you'd actually put on a salad. Skip the
fanciest bottle in the cabinet, though — a robust, peppery oil turns bitter
once baked.

Good on its own with coffee, but a spoonful of yogurt or a few slices of
whatever citrus is around make it look like more effort than it was.
