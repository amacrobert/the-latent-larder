title: King Ranch Chicken Casserole with Green Chiles
date: 2026-08-05 05:00:02 -0400
description: Shredded chicken and corn tortillas layered into a spiced sour cream sauce and baked under a blanket of melted cheddar — a Texas potluck fixture, not a can of soup in sight.
image: /assets/images/King-Ranch-Chicken-Casserole.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 40 min
tags: [chicken, baking, make-ahead, main, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 3 tbsp | butter
  - 1 | yellow onion, diced
  - 1 | green bell pepper, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 3 tbsp | all-purpose flour
  - 2 cups | chicken broth
  - 1 cup | sour cream
  - 10 oz | canned diced tomatoes with green chiles, drained
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | chili powder
  - 4 cups | shredded rotisserie chicken
  - 12 | corn tortillas, torn into large pieces
  - 3 cups | shredded sharp cheddar
  - To finish | sliced scallions
method:
  - Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the onion and bell pepper until soft, about 7 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds, then sprinkle in the flour and stir until no dry patches remain.
  - Whisk in the broth and bring to a simmer, whisking often, until slightly thickened, about 3 minutes.
  - Off the heat, stir in the sour cream, diced tomatoes with green chiles, cumin, and chili powder until smooth. Fold in the shredded chicken.
  - Spread a thin layer of the sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish, then layer a third of the torn tortillas over it.
  - Top with a third of the chicken mixture and a third of the cheddar. Repeat the layers twice more, finishing with cheese on top.
  - Bake at 375°F until bubbling at the edges and the cheese is deeply browned in spots, 30-35 minutes.
  - Rest 10 minutes before scattering with scallions and serving.
---

The from-scratch sour cream sauce does what a can of cream-of-something soup would, but it actually tastes like something — the roux gives it body, and the sour cream keeps it from turning gluey in the oven. Torn tortillas are correct here, not cut: ragged edges soak up more sauce than a clean strip does, and the dish is meant to fall apart in the scoop, not slice like a lasagna.

Rotisserie chicken keeps this to twenty minutes of prep, but leftover poached or grilled chicken breast works the same. It holds and reheats well, which makes it a fair make-ahead casserole to build the night before and bake straight from the fridge — add five minutes to the covered bake time if it's still cold in the middle.
