---
title: Crispy Air Fryer Salmon with Honey-Soy Glaze
date: 2026-07-31 17:30:03 -0400
description: Skin blistered dry-hot in a basket, then brushed with a glaze that would burn if it went in any earlier.
image: /assets/images/Crispy-Air-Fryer-Salmon-With-Honey-Soy-Glaze.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [fish, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 4 fillets | salmon, skin-on (about 6 oz each)
  - 1 tbsp | vegetable oil
  - To taste | Salt and pepper
  - 3 tbsp | soy sauce
  - 2 tbsp | honey
  - 1 tbsp | rice vinegar
  - 2 cloves | garlic, grated
  - 1 tsp | fresh ginger, grated
  - To finish | sliced scallions and sesame seeds
method:
  - Pat the salmon dry, rub with the oil, and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  - Whisk together the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, garlic, and ginger in a small saucepan.
  - Simmer over medium heat until it coats the back of a spoon, 3 to 4 minutes, then set half aside for the table.
  - Arrange the fillets skin-side down in the air fryer basket, without touching.
  - Cook at 400°F until the skin is crisp and the fish is opaque at the edges, 7 to 8 minutes.
  - Brush the tops with the glaze remaining in the pan and cook 1 minute more, just until it sets into a lacquer.
  - Brush with the reserved glaze and scatter with scallions and sesame seeds before serving.
---

The glaze goes on at the very end because honey scorches fast in that much dry heat — brush it on any earlier and you get char instead of lacquer. Keeping half back unglazed solves the problem twice: the fish gets a real coat of sauce without ever sitting under a burner.

No air fryer, no problem: broil skin-side up on a rack 4 inches from the element for the same 7 to 8 minutes, glazing in the last minute exactly as above.
