---
title: Shrimp Scampi with Linguine and Lemon
date: 2026-08-01 21:00:03 -0400
description: A garlic-and-wine butter sauce built in the same skillet the shrimp seared in, tossed with noodles in under twenty minutes.
image: /assets/images/Shrimp-Scampi-With-Linguine.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [pasta, shrimp, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 12 oz | linguine
  - 1 lb | large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  - ¼ cup | olive oil
  - 5 tbsp | butter, divided
  - 6 cloves | garlic, thinly sliced
  - ½ cup | dry white wine
  - ¼ cup | lemon juice
  - ¼ tsp | red pepper flakes
  - ¼ cup | parsley, chopped
  - To taste | salt and pepper
  - To finish | lemon wedges
method:
  - Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook the linguine until just shy of al dente, reserving 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
  - Pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper.
  - Heat the olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering, then sear the shrimp in a single layer until pink and just curled, about 1 minute per side. Transfer to a plate.
  - Lower the heat to medium, add the garlic and red pepper flakes to the same skillet, and cook until the garlic turns golden and fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Pour in the wine, scraping up the browned bits, and simmer until reduced by half.
  - Stir in the lemon juice and remaining butter, swirling the pan until the sauce turns glossy and thickened.
  - Return the shrimp to the skillet with the drained linguine, tossing and adding splashes of pasta water until the sauce clings to the noodles.
  - Scatter with parsley and serve immediately with lemon wedges.
---

The whole dish happens in one skillet: sear the shrimp first so they don't overcook while the sauce comes together, then build the sauce in the same fond. Deglaze hard enough to lift every browned bit — that's most of the flavor.

Swap the wine for an equal amount of low-sodium chicken broth with a squeeze of extra lemon if you'd rather not open a bottle for a half cup.
