---
title: Instant Pot Steel-Cut Oats with Brown Sugar and Cinnamon
date: 2026-08-07 12:00:02 -0400
description: Steel-cut oats under pressure for six minutes, so the chewy, unhulled oat comes out creamy instead of taking thirty minutes of stovetop stirring.
image: /assets/images/Instant-Pot-Steel-Cut-Oats.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 6 min
tags: [breakfast, vegetarian, quick, make-ahead, gluten-free]
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp | butter
  - 1½ cups | steel-cut oats
  - 4 cups | water
  - ½ cup | milk, plus more to serve
  - ¼ cup | packed brown sugar
  - 1 tsp | cinnamon
  - ½ tsp | kosher salt
  - To finish | a pat of butter and extra brown sugar
method:
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté, melt the butter, and toast the steel-cut oats for 2 minutes, stirring, until they smell nutty.
  - Add the water, milk, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt, and scrape up anything stuck to the bottom of the pot.
  - Seal the lid and cook on high pressure for 6 minutes.
  - Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick-release whatever remains.
  - Stir well — the oats will look thin at first and thicken into a creamy porridge within a minute or two.
  - Spoon into bowls and top with a pat of butter, a splash of milk, and more brown sugar.
---

Steel-cut oats are the chopped, unhulled kernel, not the flattened rolled oat — they hold a distinct chew that turning them into the stovetop's usual thirty-minute simmer would eventually erode anyway, so the six-minute pressure-cooked version isn't a shortcut so much as the right amount of cooking. Scraping the pot bottom after adding the liquid matters more here than in most pressure-cooker recipes: any oats or sugar stuck underneath the toasting step can trigger a burn warning before the pot ever comes to pressure.

This keeps well — portion the extra into jars and reheat with a splash of milk through the week. Swap the brown sugar and cinnamon for a spoon of pesto and grated parmesan if you want it savory instead.
