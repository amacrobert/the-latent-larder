---
title: Chewy Oatmeal Raisin Cookies
date: 2026-08-03 18:00:02 -0400
description: Old-fashioned oats, plump raisins, and enough cinnamon to matter — the cookie the chocolate chip one always stands next to.
image: /assets/images/Chewy-Oatmeal-Raisin-Cookies.webp
servings: 24
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 12 min
tags: [dessert, baking, quick]
ingredients:
  - 1 cup | raisins
  - 1½ cups | flour
  - 1 tsp | baking soda
  - 1 tsp | ground cinnamon
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - 1 cup | unsalted butter, softened
  - 1 cup | brown sugar, packed
  - ½ cup | granulated sugar
  - 2 | eggs
  - 1 tsp | vanilla extract
  - 3 cups | old-fashioned rolled oats
method:
  - Cover the raisins with hot water and let them sit for 10 minutes, then drain well.
  - Whisk together the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a bowl and set aside.
  - Beat the softened butter with the brown sugar and granulated sugar until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, until fully combined.
  - Add the dry ingredients and mix on low just until no streaks of flour remain.
  - Fold in the oats and drained raisins.
  - Scoop into 2-tablespoon mounds onto parchment-lined sheet pans, spaced 2 inches apart.
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and bake until the edges are golden and the centers still look soft, 11 to 13 minutes.
  - Let cool on the pan for 5 minutes before moving to a rack.
---

Soaking the raisins in hot water before they go in the dough keeps them from pulling moisture out of the cookie as it bakes — skip that step and you get dry, leathery specks instead of plump ones. Old-fashioned oats hold their shape better than quick oats here; quick oats will still work, but the cookie turns denser and loses the chew.

Pull the pan while the centers still look underdone — oatmeal cookies firm up a lot as they cool, and a cookie that looks done in the oven is a cookie that's overbaked by the time it hits the rack. Swap the raisins for dried cranberries or chocolate chips if you want a variation, same method throughout.
