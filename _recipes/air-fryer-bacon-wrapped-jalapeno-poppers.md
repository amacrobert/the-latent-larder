---
title: Air Fryer Bacon-Wrapped Jalapeño Poppers
date: 2026-08-04 03:00:03 -0400
description: Halved jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese and cheddar, wrapped in bacon, and air-fried until the bacon crisps and the peppers blister.
image: /assets/images/Air-Fryer-Bacon-Wrapped-Jalapeno-Poppers.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 12 min
tags: [appetizer, spicy, quick, cheese, pork]
ingredients:
  - 8 large | jalapeños, halved and seeded
  - 8 oz | cream cheese, softened
  - 1 cup | sharp cheddar, shredded
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - ½ tsp | smoked paprika
  - 8 slices | bacon, halved crosswise
  - To finish | chopped chives
method:
  - Beat the cream cheese, cheddar, garlic, and smoked paprika together until evenly combined.
  - Pack a heaping spoonful of the cheese mixture into each jalapeño half, mounding it slightly above the rim.
  - Wrap each stuffed half tightly with a piece of bacon, overlapping the ends and securing with a toothpick if it won't hold on its own.
  - Arrange in a single layer in the air fryer basket, cut side up, without crowding.
  - Air fry at 375°F for 10 to 12 minutes, until the bacon is crisp and rendered and the peppers have blistered at the edges.
  - Let rest 5 minutes before serving — the cheese filling is lava-hot straight out of the basket.
  - Scatter with chives.
---

The bacon needs to actually crisp, not just cook through, or the poppers turn soggy under the cheese — if yours are browning slower than the peppers are softening, finish them under a hot broiler for the last minute instead of running the air fryer longer.

Seed the jalapeños all the way, including the white ribs, if you want these mild enough for a crowd; leave a few ribs in on half the batch for people who want the heat. No air fryer works too — bake at 400°F on a wire rack over a sheet pan for 18 to 20 minutes.
