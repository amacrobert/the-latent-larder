---
title: Huevos Rancheros with Crispy Tortillas
date: 2026-08-06 00:00:03 -0400
description: Fried eggs over crisped corn tortillas, ladled with a simmered tomato-chile ranchero sauce and warm refried beans.
image: /assets/images/Huevos-Rancheros-With-Crispy-Tortillas.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [breakfast, mexican, vegetarian, gluten-free, quick]
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp | vegetable oil, divided
  - 1 | white onion, diced
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 | jalapeño, seeded and diced
  - 1 can (14 oz) | fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  - ½ tsp | ground cumin
  - To taste | salt
  - 8 | corn tortillas
  - 1 can (16 oz) | refried beans
  - 4 | large eggs
  - To finish | crumbled queso fresco
  - To finish | cilantro leaves
  - 1 | lime, cut into wedges
  - To finish | sliced avocado
method:
  - Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a saucepan over medium and cook the onion until softened, about 5 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and jalapeño and cook until fragrant, 1 minute.
  - Stir in the tomatoes and cumin, simmer until slightly thickened, 8 to 10 minutes, then blend smooth (or leave chunky) and season with salt. Keep warm.
  - Warm the refried beans in a small pot, thinning with a splash of water if needed. Keep warm.
  - Wipe out a skillet, heat the remaining oil over medium-high, and fry the tortillas one at a time until spotted brown and crisp at the edges, about 1 minute per side. Drain on paper towels.
  - In the same skillet, fry the eggs to your liking, whites set and yolks still soft.
  - Spread beans over each tortilla, top with a fried egg, and spoon ranchero sauce over the top.
  - Finish with queso fresco, cilantro, and avocado, and serve with lime wedges.
---

The sauce is the one thing to get right — simmer it until it coats a spoon, because a thin sauce turns the crisp tortilla to mush before anyone gets a fork in. Blending it is optional; left chunky it's closer to a fresh salsa, blended it clings better.

Fry the tortillas in batches and hold them on a wire rack, not stacked on paper towels, or the bottom ones steam soft again. If you're feeding a crowd, everything up through the sauce and beans holds well on low heat — fry the eggs to order.
