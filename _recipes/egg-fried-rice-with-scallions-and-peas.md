---
title: Egg Fried Rice with Scallions and Peas
date: 2026-07-28 14:02:33 -0400
description: Day-old rice separated to distinct grains in a screaming-hot wok, faster than delivery and better with whatever's in the crisper.
image: /assets/images/Egg-Fried-Rice-With-Scallions-And-Peas.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [rice, vegetarian, quick, side]
ingredients:
  - 3 cups | cold, cooked rice, a day old
  - 2 tbsp | neutral oil, divided
  - 2 | eggs, beaten
  - 3 | scallions, sliced, white and green kept apart
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - ½ cup | frozen peas
  - 2 tbsp | soy sauce
  - 1 tsp | toasted sesame oil
  - To taste | white pepper
method:
  - Heat 1 tbsp of the neutral oil in a wok or wide skillet over high heat until it shimmers.
  - Pour in the eggs and scramble fast, breaking them into small curds, then tip onto a plate.
  - Add the remaining oil, scallion whites, and garlic, and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Add the rice, breaking up any clumps with the back of a spatula, and press it into an even layer.
  - Leave it alone for a minute so the bottom starts to catch, then toss.
  - Add the peas, soy sauce, and scrambled egg, tossing until everything's hot and the grains have separated.
  - Off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and scallion greens, and season with white pepper.
---

Cold, day-old rice is not a suggestion — fresh rice is too wet and turns the whole pan gummy. Spread a fresh batch on a sheet pan to cool fast if you're starting from scratch.

Treat the peas and scallions as the minimum, not the recipe: diced ham, leftover roast chicken, or a handful of shrimp turn this from a side into dinner.
