---
title: Sheet-Pan BBQ Chicken Pizza with Smoked Gouda
date: 2026-08-08 12:00:02 -0400
description: Rotisserie chicken and smoked gouda over a barbecue-sauce base, baked hot and fast on a preheated sheet pan instead of a stone.
image: /assets/images/Sheet-Pan-BBQ-Chicken-Pizza-With-Smoked-Gouda.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [pizza, chicken, quick, main, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | pizza dough, at room temperature
  - 2 cups | shredded rotisserie chicken
  - ¾ cup | barbecue sauce, divided
  - 8 oz | low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
  - 4 oz | smoked gouda, shredded
  - ½ | red onion, thinly sliced
  - A handful | cilantro leaves
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil, for the pan
method:
  - Place a rimmed sheet pan in the oven and heat to 475°F, letting the pan heat through for at least 15 minutes.
  - Toss the shredded chicken with ¼ cup of the barbecue sauce until evenly coated.
  - On a well-floured sheet of parchment, stretch and press the dough into a rough rectangle to fit the pan.
  - Pull the hot pan from the oven, brush it with the olive oil, and slide the dough on, parchment and all.
  - Spread the remaining barbecue sauce over the dough, leaving a bare ½-inch border, then scatter with the mozzarella and gouda.
  - Top with the barbecue chicken and red onion.
  - Bake until the crust is deeply browned on the bottom and the cheese is bubbling and browned in spots, 12 to 15 minutes.
  - Scatter with cilantro and slice.
---

The preheated pan is what stands in for a steel here — sliding the dough straight onto hot metal sets and browns the bottom before the toppings have a chance to steam it soft. Go easy on the sauce for the same reason; a thick layer under the cheese never fully cooks off.

Leftover pulled pork or shredded turkey works as well as the rotisserie chicken, and sharp cheddar is a fine stand-in for the gouda if that's what's in the drawer.
