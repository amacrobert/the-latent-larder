---
title: Chicken Enchiladas with Salsa Verde
date: 2026-08-03 09:00:03 -0400
description: Shredded chicken and sour cream rolled into corn tortillas, drowned in tangy tomatillo salsa, and baked until the cheese browns in spots.
image: /assets/images/Chicken-Enchiladas-With-Salsa-Verde.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [chicken, baking, main, cheese]
ingredients:
  - 3 cups | shredded rotisserie chicken
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1 | small onion, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - ½ cup | sour cream
  - 8 oz | shredded Monterey Jack
  - 12 | corn tortillas
  - 2 cups | salsa verde
  - A handful | cilantro, chopped
  - To finish | sour cream and lime wedges
method:
  - Heat the oil in a skillet over medium and cook the onion until soft and translucent, about 6 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cumin and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Off the heat, stir in the shredded chicken, sour cream, and half the cheese until the mixture holds together.
  - Spread a thin layer of salsa verde over the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  - Warm the tortillas until pliable, fill each with the chicken mixture, roll tightly, and lay seam-side down in the dish.
  - Pour the remaining salsa verde over the top and scatter with the rest of the cheese.
  - Bake at 425°F until the sauce bubbles at the edges and the cheese is browned in spots, 20-25 minutes.
  - Rest 5 minutes, then finish with cilantro, sour cream, and lime before serving.
---

Warm the tortillas before you fill them — cold corn tortillas crack the second you try to roll them, and a torn enchilada bleeds its filling into the sauce. Stirring the sour cream into the chicken while it's off the heat does double duty: it binds the filling and keeps it from drying out under twenty minutes of oven heat.

Jarred salsa verde works fine here, but a homemade roasted tomatillo sauce is worth it if you have a broiler free. Leftover poached or grilled chicken breast is a fine swap for rotisserie — just make sure it's shredded fine enough to roll without tearing the tortilla.
