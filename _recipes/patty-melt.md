---
title: Griddled Patty Melt with Caramelized Onions
date: 2026-08-10 03:00:02 -0400
description: A ground beef patty and melted Swiss pressed between buttered rye, griddled like a grilled cheese so every bite is crisp on the outside and molten in the middle.
image: /assets/images/Griddled-Patty-Melt-With-Caramelized-Onions.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 30 min
tags: [beef, sandwich, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp | butter
  - 2 | yellow onions, thinly sliced
  - 1½ lb | ground beef (80/20)
  - 8 slices | rye bread
  - 8 slices | Swiss cheese
  - 4 tbsp | butter, softened, for the bread
  - To taste | kosher salt and black pepper
method:
  - Melt the butter in a skillet over medium-low heat and cook the onions, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and jammy, 20 to 25 minutes. Set aside.
  - Divide the beef into 4 oval patties about the length of the bread and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  - Wipe out the skillet, raise the heat to medium-high, and sear the patties until deeply browned, about 3 minutes per side, then set aside.
  - Butter one side of each slice of rye.
  - Build each sandwich butter-side out: bread, a slice of cheese, a patty, a portion of caramelized onions, another slice of cheese, bread.
  - Griddle over medium-low heat, pressing gently with a spatula, until the bottom is deeply browned, about 4 minutes.
  - Flip and griddle the second side until browned and the cheese has fully melted, about 4 minutes more.
  - Cut in half and serve while the cheese is still pulling.
---

The patty gets seared hard and hot first, then finished by the residual heat while the sandwich griddles — cook it through on the flattop instead and the bread burns long before the middle catches up.

Two skillets speed this up: one holding heat for the onions while the other sears patties. No rye on hand, a hearty sourdough griddles just as well, though the diner argument is stronger with rye.
