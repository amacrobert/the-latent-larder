---
title: Grilled Chicken Satay with Peanut Sauce
date: 2026-07-31 06:00:03 -0400
description: Thigh meat marinated in curry and fish sauce, skewered thin, and charred hard over direct heat for dunking.
image: /assets/images/Grilled-Chicken-Satay-With-Peanut-Sauce.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [chicken, grilled, appetizer, quick, spicy]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | chicken thighs, boneless, skinless, sliced into strips
  - ¼ cup | soy sauce
  - 2 tbsp | fish sauce
  - 2 tbsp | brown sugar
  - 1 tbsp | curry powder
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp | fresh ginger, grated
  - 8 | wooden skewers, soaked
  - ½ cup | natural peanut butter
  - ¼ cup | coconut milk
  - 2 tbsp | lime juice
  - 1 tbsp | soy sauce
  - 1 | red chile, minced
  - Water | to thin
method:
  - Whisk the soy sauce, fish sauce, brown sugar, curry powder, garlic, and ginger until the sugar dissolves.
  - Add the chicken strips and toss to coat, then marinate in the fridge at least 1 hour or overnight.
  - Thread each strip onto a soaked skewer, weaving back and forth so it lies flat and cooks evenly.
  - Whisk the peanut butter, coconut milk, lime juice, soy sauce, and chile together, thinning with water a tablespoon at a time until the sauce drips slowly off the whisk.
  - Grill the skewers over direct high heat, turning once, until charred at the edges and the chicken is opaque all the way through, 3 to 4 minutes a side.
  - Serve hot off the grill with the peanut sauce alongside for dipping.
---

The marinade is doing double duty — it seasons the chicken and, with the sugar in it, is what gives the edges that char before the inside overcooks. Thigh meat is worth the extra trim over breast here; it stays juicy through the direct heat that a thinner cut can't take.

Thin the peanut sauce more than looks right at first — it firms up as it sits, and a stiff sauce just clumps on the skewer instead of coating it. Leftover sauce keeps a week in the fridge and is good on cold noodles or a bowl of rice.
