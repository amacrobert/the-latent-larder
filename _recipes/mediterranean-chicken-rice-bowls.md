---
title: Mediterranean Chicken and Rice Bowls with Tzatziki
date: 2026-08-01 12:01:42 -0400
description: Marinated chicken thighs, lemon rice, and a garlic-yogurt sauce, built to split across containers and eat all week.
image: /assets/images/Mediterranean-Chicken-Rice-Bowls-With-Tzatziki.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [chicken, rice, quick, main, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | chicken thighs, boneless, skinless
  - ¼ cup | olive oil, divided
  - 3 tbsp | lemon juice, divided
  - 4 cloves | garlic, minced, divided
  - 1 tbsp | dried oregano
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | paprika
  - 1½ tsp | kosher salt, divided
  - 1½ cups | long-grain rice
  - 2¼ cups | chicken broth
  - 1 cup | plain Greek yogurt
  - ½ | cucumber, grated and squeezed dry, divided
  - 1 cup | cherry tomatoes, halved
  - ¼ cup | red onion, thinly sliced
  - ½ cup | crumbled feta
  - A handful | parsley, chopped, to finish
method:
  - Whisk 3 tbsp of the olive oil, 2 tbsp of the lemon juice, 3 cloves of the garlic, the oregano, cumin, paprika, and 1 tsp of the salt. Add the chicken and turn to coat, then marinate at least 30 minutes or up to overnight.
  - Combine the rice, broth, and remaining salt in a pot. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer over low heat until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender, about 18 minutes. Let it sit covered off the heat for 5 minutes, then fluff.
  - While the rice cooks, stir the yogurt, remaining garlic, remaining lemon juice, remaining olive oil, and half the grated cucumber together. Season with salt to taste.
  - Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the chicken until deeply browned and the thickest part reads 165°F, 6 to 7 minutes a side, then rest 5 minutes and slice.
  - Divide the rice among bowls and top with the sliced chicken, tomatoes, red onion, remaining cucumber, and feta. Finish with a spoonful of tzatziki and the parsley.
---

The marinade is short on time but long on acid and salt, which is what actually penetrates in half an hour — don't stretch it into a rub-and-sear, or the chicken cooks up seasoned only on the surface. Thighs also forgive a few extra minutes in the skillet better than breast would if you're timing it against the rice.

This is built to portion into containers: rice on the bottom, chicken and raw vegetables on top, tzatziki packed separately so nothing turns the rice soggy by day three. Swap in warm pita and skip the rice entirely if you'd rather eat it as a wrap.
