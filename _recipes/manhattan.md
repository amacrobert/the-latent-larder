description: Rye and sweet vermouth stirred cold over ice and strained up — the drink every shaken cocktail in this larder isn't.

servings: 1
prep_time: 5 min
tags: [drink, quick]
ingredients:
  - 2 oz | rye whiskey
  - 1 oz | sweet vermouth
  - 2 dashes | Angostura bitters
  - 1 | brandied cherry
  - To fill | ice
method:
  - Fill a mixing glass two-thirds with ice.
  - Add the rye, vermouth, and bitters.
  - Stir for 30 seconds, until the outside of the glass turns properly cold.
  - Strain into a chilled coupe.
  - Drop in the brandied cherry and serve.
---

Stir, don't shake. Shaking beats air into the drink and clouds it with tiny ice shards that water it down fast; stirring chills it just as cold while keeping the texture silky and the dilution under control. Thirty seconds in the mixing glass is enough — the outside should feel properly cold to the touch, not just cool.

Rye keeps the drink dry and a little spicy; swap in bourbon for a rounder, sweeter version, which is really a different drink wearing the same name. Either way, spend the extra dollar on a real brandied cherry — the fluorescent jarred kind is the fastest way to ruin an otherwise good cocktail.
