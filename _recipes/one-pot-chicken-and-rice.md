---
title: One-Pot Chicken and Rice with Lemon
date: 2026-07-31 22:30:02 -0400
description: Chicken thighs seared for color, then the rice finishes cooking in the same skillet in the drippings and stock.
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 35 min
tags: [chicken, rice, main, quick]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
  - 2 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1 | small yellow onion, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1½ cups | long-grain white rice
  - 2¾ cups | chicken broth
  - 1 | lemon, zested and juiced
  - 1 tsp | dried thyme
  - 1 cup | frozen peas
  - To finish | chopped parsley
method:
  - Season the chicken thighs all over with salt and pepper.
  - Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or shallow Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
  - Sear the chicken until deeply browned, about 5 minutes per side, then transfer to a plate (it won't be cooked through yet).
  - Add the onion to the drippings and cook until softened, about 4 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Stir in the rice, toasting until the edges turn translucent, about 1 minute.
  - Pour in the broth, lemon zest and juice, and thyme, scraping up any browned bits, and bring to a simmer.
  - Nestle the chicken back into the skillet along with any collected juices.
  - Cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed, about 18 minutes.
  - Scatter the peas over the top, cover, and let sit off the heat for 5 minutes until they're warmed through.
  - Fluff the rice around the chicken, scatter with parsley, and serve straight from the skillet.
---

Thighs are the right cut here, not breasts — they can sit in the skillet for the full eighteen minutes the rice needs without drying out, and the fond they leave behind while searing is most of the seasoning in the dish. Don't skip that sear even though the chicken goes back in undercooked; the crust is where the color and a good deal of the flavor come from.

Swap the peas for a couple of handfuls of baby spinach stirred in at the end if that's what's on hand, and add a diced bell pepper with the onion for more vegetable in the pot.
