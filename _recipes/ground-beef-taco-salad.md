---
title: Ground Beef Taco Salad with Cumin-Lime Crema
date: 2026-08-01 04:00:03 -0400
description: Browned beef and crushed tortilla chips over crisp romaine, pulled together with a tangy cumin-lime crema instead of a bottled dressing.
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [beef, salad, quick, main, spicy]
ingredients:
  - ½ cup | sour cream
  - 2 tbsp | mayonnaise
  - 1 | lime, zested and juiced
  - ½ tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 lb | ground beef (80/20)
  - 1 | small onion, diced
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 tbsp | chili powder
  - ½ tsp | smoked paprika
  - ¼ cup | water
  - 1 | large head romaine, chopped
  - 1 can | black beans, drained and rinsed (15 oz)
  - 1 cup | cherry tomatoes, halved
  - 1 | avocado, diced
  - 1 cup | shredded cheddar
  - 2 cups | tortilla chips, crushed
  - To taste | kosher salt
method:
  - Whisk the sour cream, mayonnaise, lime zest and juice, and cumin in a small bowl and season with salt; refrigerate until you're ready to serve.
  - Heat a large dry skillet over medium-high and add the ground beef in an even layer; let it sit undisturbed for 2-3 minutes until the underside is deeply browned before breaking it up.
  - Add the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and the beef is cooked through, about 5 minutes.
  - Stir in the garlic, chili powder, and smoked paprika and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Add the water and simmer until it's mostly absorbed and the beef looks glossy rather than wet, about 2 minutes; season with salt and let cool for a few minutes.
  - Pile the romaine on a large platter or in a wide bowl and scatter the black beans, tomatoes, avocado, and cheddar over the top.
  - Spoon the warm beef over the salad, crush the tortilla chips over everything, and drizzle with the cumin-lime crema just before serving.
---

Let the beef cool for a few minutes before it hits the lettuce — straight off the heat it wilts the romaine into something closer to a warm slaw than a salad. The crema is the part worth not skipping: it does the job of a dressing without the oil-and-vinegar split that a squeeze bottle of ranch never quite avoids.

Swap in ground turkey or shredded rotisserie chicken if you want it leaner. Crush the tortilla chips at the last second, not in the bowl ahead of time, or they'll go soft under the crema before anyone's had a bite.
