---
title: Grilled Corn and Tomato Pizza
date: 2026-07-27 23:45:46 -0400
description: Dough charred straight on the grill grates, topped in a rush while the first side is still hot enough to melt the cheese.
image: /assets/images/Grilled-Corn-And-Tomato-Pizza.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [pizza, grilled, vegetarian, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | pizza dough, at room temperature
  - 2 tbsp | olive oil, plus more for the grill
  - 2 ears | corn, shucked
  - 1 cup | cherry tomatoes, halved
  - 8 oz | fresh mozzarella, torn
  - 2 cloves | garlic, grated
  - A handful | basil leaves, torn
  - To finish | flaky salt and red pepper flakes
method:
  - Heat a grill to high and brush the grates with oil.
  - Grill the corn, turning occasionally, until charred in spots and just tender, about 8 minutes, then let it cool and cut the kernels from the cob.
  - Divide the dough in half and stretch each piece into a rough 10-inch round on a floured surface.
  - Brush both sides of the dough with olive oil and lay it directly on the grill grates.
  - Grill until the underside is charred in spots and the dough has puffed and set, about 2 minutes, then flip with tongs.
  - Working quickly, scatter the grilled side with garlic, mozzarella, corn, and tomatoes.
  - Close the lid and grill until the cheese has melted and the bottom is crisp, 3 to 4 minutes.
  - Slide onto a board, scatter with basil, flaky salt, and red pepper flakes, and slice.
---

The dough only gets one real window to cook through before it scorches, so have every topping shucked, torn, and within arm's reach before it hits the grates. A pizza peel helps, but a flat baking sheet works for the slide-on, slide-off.

No grill doesn't mean no pizza — a screaming-hot cast-iron skillet under the broiler gets most of the way there, just watch the top instead of the bottom. Swap the corn and tomatoes for whatever's heavy at the market; the garlic and mozzarella base holds up under almost anything.
