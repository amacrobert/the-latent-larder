---
title: Chewy Peanut Butter Cookies with Sea Salt
date: 2026-07-31 19:00:03 -0400
description: More peanut butter than flour, pressed with a fork so the dense dough has somewhere to spread.
image: /assets/images/Chewy-Peanut-Butter-Cookies.webp
servings: 24
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 12 min
tags: [dessert, baking, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1¼ cups | creamy peanut butter
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter, softened
  - 1 cup | brown sugar, packed
  - ½ cup | granulated sugar, plus more for rolling
  - 2 | eggs
  - 1 tsp | vanilla extract
  - 2½ cups | flour
  - 1 tsp | baking soda
  - ½ tsp | baking powder
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - To finish | flaky salt
method:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and line two sheet pans with parchment.
  - Beat the butter, peanut butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar together until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, until smooth.
  - Whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl, then fold into the wet ingredients until just combined.
  - Roll the dough into 1½-inch balls, roll each in granulated sugar, and space 2 inches apart on the sheet pans.
  - Press each ball twice with a fork in a crosshatch, flattening it to about ½ inch.
  - Bake until the edges are set and just turning gold but the centers still look soft, 10 to 12 minutes.
  - Sprinkle with flaky salt while warm, then cool on the pan for 5 minutes before moving to a rack.
---

Peanut butter dough is stiffer than a standard butter cookie and won't spread on its own, which is what the fork crosshatch is actually for — it's not decoration, it's the only thing flattening the cookie before the edges set. Pull them while the centers still look slightly underdone; they firm up completely as they cool, and a cookie that looks fully baked in the oven is overbaked by the time it hits the rack.

Use a shelf-stable, no-stir creamy peanut butter — natural peanut butter's separated oil throws off the ratio and bakes up greasy. Chunky peanut butter works if you want texture in the crumb instead of just on top.
