---
title: Red Wine-Braised Beef Short Ribs
date: 2026-07-27
description: Bone-in ribs seared hard, then given three hours in the oven until the meat pulls off the bone on its own.
image: /assets/images/Red-Wine-Braised-Beef-Short-Ribs.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 3 hr
tags: [beef, slow, main]
ingredients:
  - 4 lb | bone-in beef short ribs
  - To taste | kosher salt and black pepper
  - 2 tbsp | neutral oil
  - 1 large | onion, diced
  - 2 | carrots, diced
  - 2 stalks | celery, diced
  - 4 cloves | garlic, smashed
  - 2 tbsp | tomato paste
  - 2 cups | dry red wine
  - 2 cups | beef stock
  - 4 sprigs | thyme
  - 2 | bay leaves
method:
  - Season the short ribs heavily with salt and pepper on all sides.
  - Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over high heat and sear the ribs until deeply browned on every side, about 12 minutes total, then transfer to a plate.
  - Lower the heat to medium, add the onion, carrots, and celery, and cook until softened, about 8 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook until the paste darkens, about 2 minutes.
  - Pour in the red wine and scrape up the browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pot, then simmer until reduced by half, about 5 minutes.
  - Return the ribs to the pot, add the stock, thyme, and bay leaves, and bring to a simmer.
  - Cover and transfer to a 325°F oven; braise until the meat is fork-tender and pulling from the bone, about 3 hours.
  - Lift the ribs onto a platter and tent with foil.
  - Skim the fat from the braising liquid, discard the thyme stems and bay leaves, and simmer on the stovetop until thickened enough to coat a spoon, about 10 minutes.
  - Spoon the sauce over the ribs to serve.
---

The sear is what the sauce is built on, so don't rush it — a pale rib gives you a thin, winey liquid instead of something dark and savory. Once it's in the oven, leave it alone; short ribs punish stirring and reward patience.

Serve over something that can soak up the sauce — mashed potatoes or polenta both work. This is better the day after: chill it overnight, lift off the hardened fat cap, and reheat gently before serving.
