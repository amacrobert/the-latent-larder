---
title: Red Wine-Poached Pears with Mascarpone
date: 2026-07-30 12:30:03 -0400
description: Pears simmered in spiced red wine until the color bleeds all the way through, then topped with mascarpone for a dinner-party dessert that needs no oven.
image: /assets/images/Red-Wine-Poached-Pears-With-Mascarpone.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 35 min
tags: [dessert, fruit, make-ahead, slow]
ingredients:
  - 4 | firm pears, peeled, stems on
  - 2 cups | dry red wine
  - ½ cup | sugar
  - 1 | cinnamon stick
  - 2 | whole cloves
  - 1 strip | orange zest
  - ½ cup | mascarpone, softened
  - 1 tbsp | honey
method:
  - Combine the wine, sugar, cinnamon, cloves, and orange zest in a pot just wide enough to hold the pears standing up; bring to a simmer, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  - Stand the pears in the liquid, cover, and simmer gently, turning them every few minutes, until a knife slides into the flesh with no resistance, 25 to 30 minutes.
  - Lift the pears out onto a plate and raise the heat under the liquid, reducing it until it coats the back of a spoon.
  - Pour the syrup over the pears and chill until fully cold, at least 2 hours.
  - Whip the mascarpone with a spoonful of the cold syrup and the honey until it loosens into a soft dollop.
  - Stand each pear in a shallow bowl, spoon over the remaining syrup, and top with the mascarpone.
---

Pick pears that are ripe enough to smell sweet but still firm to the squeeze — anything soft will collapse before the color has time to travel to the center. Bosc holds its shape best; Anjou works if that's what's at the market.

The syrup keeps in the fridge for a week and only gets better, so poach the pears a day ahead if you're serving them for company. No mascarpone on hand, crème fraîche or a scoop of vanilla ice cream does the same job of cutting the sweetness.
