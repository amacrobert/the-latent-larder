---
title: Beer-Battered Fish Tacos with Chipotle Crema
date: 2026-07-28 09:46:09 -0400
description: Cod fried in a beer batter until it crackles, folded into warm tortillas with slaw and a smoky crema.
image: /assets/images/Beer-Battered-Fish-Tacos-With-Chipotle-Crema.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [fish, fried, quick, main, spicy]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | cod or halibut, cut into strips
  - 1 cup | all-purpose flour, divided
  - 1 tsp | baking powder
  - 1 tsp | chili powder
  - ¾ cup | cold beer (lager)
  - 1 | egg
  - Vegetable oil, for frying
  - 8 | small corn tortillas
  - 2 cups | green cabbage, thinly sliced
  - ¼ cup | mayonnaise
  - 2 tbsp | chipotle in adobo, minced
  - 1 | lime, juiced and zested
  - To finish | cilantro and lime wedges
  - Salt, to taste
method:
  - Whisk ½ cup of the flour with the baking powder, chili powder, and a pinch of salt, then whisk in the egg and cold beer until just smooth — a few lumps are fine.
  - Toss the cabbage with the lime zest, half the lime juice, and a pinch of salt, and set aside to soften while you fry.
  - Stir the mayonnaise, chipotle, and remaining lime juice together for the crema.
  - Heat 1 inch of oil in a heavy pot until it reaches 375°F.
  - Pat the fish dry, dredge each strip through the remaining flour, then coat in the batter, letting the excess drip off.
  - Fry in batches until the crust is deep gold and the strips float to the surface, 3 to 4 minutes, then drain on a rack.
  - Warm the tortillas directly over a burner flame until they blister in spots.
  - Fill each tortilla with fish and slaw, drizzle with crema, and finish with cilantro and a lime wedge.
---

The batter only works cold — beer straight from the fridge, mixed just before frying, so the carbonation is still fighting to get out when the strips hit the oil. That's what gives you a crust that shatters instead of one that goes leathery as it sits. Watch the oil temperature more than the clock: too cool and the batter drinks oil instead of crisping.

Shrimp fries just as well in the same batter if you want a change — pat them dry and give them a minute less. Whatever fish you use, fry it in small batches; crowd the pot and the temperature drops fast enough that the second half never catches up.
