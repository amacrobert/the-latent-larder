---
title: Grilled Lamb Kofta with Tahini Yogurt
date: 2026-07-27
description: Ground lamb packed hard onto skewers and charred over direct heat, done before the coals even need tending.
image: /assets/images/Grilled-Lamb-Kofta-With-Tahini-Yogurt.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [lamb, grilled, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | ground lamb
  - 1 | small onion, grated and squeezed dry
  - 4 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 2 tsp | ground cumin
  - 2 tsp | ground coriander
  - ½ tsp | ground cinnamon
  - ¼ cup | chopped parsley
  - 2 tbsp | chopped mint
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ tsp | black pepper
  - 8 | wooden skewers, soaked
  - ¾ cup | plain yogurt
  - 3 tbsp | tahini
  - 1 | lemon, juiced
  - To finish | flaky salt and sumac
method:
  - Combine the lamb, onion, garlic, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, parsley, mint, salt, and pepper by hand until evenly mixed but no longer streaky.
  - Divide into 8 portions and mold each around a skewer into a tight oblong log, pressing out any air pockets.
  - Chill the skewers at least 15 minutes, until firm enough to hold together on the grill.
  - Whisk the yogurt, tahini, and lemon juice with a splash of water until pourable, then season with salt.
  - Grill the kofta over direct high heat, turning every couple minutes, until charred in spots and no longer pink at the center, about 8 minutes total.
  - Spoon the tahini yogurt onto plates and lay the kofta on top, finished with flaky salt and sumac.
---

Squeezing the grated onion dry is what keeps the meat from turning to paste — skip that step and the kofta slide right off the skewer over the coals. Keep the mixture cold until the moment it hits the grill; a warm log won't hold its shape.

Ground beef works in place of lamb if that's what's around, though the fat content should stay close to 80/20 or the skewers dry out fast. Wrap the finished kofta in warm pita with sliced cucumber if you want it to travel.
