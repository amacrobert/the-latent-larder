title: Grilled Reuben Sandwich with Russian Dressing
date: 2026-07-30 14:30:03 -0400
description: Corned beef, sauerkraut, and Swiss between griddled rye — a deli-counter sandwich that's actually better built at home over low, patient heat.
image: /assets/images/Grilled-Reuben-Sandwich-With-Russian-Dressing.webp
servings: 2
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [sandwich, beef, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 4 slices | rye bread
  - ½ lb | corned beef, thinly sliced
  - 4 slices | Swiss cheese
  - 1 cup | sauerkraut, well drained
  - ¼ cup | mayonnaise
  - 2 tbsp | ketchup
  - 1 tbsp | sweet relish
  - 2 tbsp | butter, softened
method:
  - Stir the mayonnaise, ketchup, and relish together to make the Russian dressing.
  - Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  - Layer each sandwich butter-side out: bread, cheese, corned beef, sauerkraut, a spoonful of dressing, cheese, bread.
  - Griddle in a skillet over medium-low heat, pressing gently with a spatula, until the bottom is deeply browned, about 4 minutes.
  - Flip and griddle the second side until browned and the cheese has fully melted, about 4 minutes more.
  - Cut on the diagonal and serve while the cheese is still pulling.
---

Wet sauerkraut is the whole problem with a bad Reuben — it steams the bread from the inside before the outside ever crisps. Squeeze it dry in a kitchen towel first, harder than feels reasonable, and cook over medium-low rather than medium: the cheese needs the extra minute to fully melt before the bread scorches.

No rye on hand, marble rye works fine and looks better on a plate. Swap the corned beef for pastrami and you've made a different sandwich with the same argument for existing — either way, a pickle spear on the side is not optional.
