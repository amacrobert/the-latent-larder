title: Tuna Noodle Casserole with Crispy Potato Chip Topping
date: 2026-08-10 12:00:03 -0400
description: Canned tuna and egg noodles bound in a from-scratch mushroom cream sauce, finished under a crust of crushed potato chips instead of a can of soup.
image: /assets/images/Tuna-Noodle-Casserole.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 30 min
tags: [fish, noodles, baking, main, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 8 oz | egg noodles
  - 4 tbsp | butter, divided
  - 8 oz | cremini mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 | yellow onion, diced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - ¼ cup | all-purpose flour
  - 2 cups | chicken broth
  - 1 cup | whole milk
  - 2 (5 oz) cans | tuna, drained
  - 1 cup | frozen peas
  - 1 tsp | lemon zest
  - To taste | salt and pepper
  - 2 cups | kettle-cooked potato chips, crushed
method:
  - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the egg noodles until just shy of al dente, a minute or two less than the package says. Drain and set aside.
  - Melt 2 tbsp butter in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until they release their liquid, the liquid evaporates, and the mushrooms start to brown, about 8 minutes.
  - Add the onion and the remaining 2 tbsp butter and cook until the onion softens, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Sprinkle in the flour and stir until it coats everything and no dry patches remain, about 1 minute.
  - Whisk in the broth and milk. Bring to a simmer, whisking often, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 5 minutes.
  - Flake in the tuna and stir in the peas and lemon zest. Season with salt and pepper, then fold in the drained noodles. Transfer everything to a greased 9x13-inch baking dish.
  - Scatter the crushed potato chips evenly over the top.
  - Bake at 375°F until the sauce bubbles at the edges and the chips are deep golden, 20 to 25 minutes.
---

A real mushroom béchamel does what the can of soup would, without tasting like tin — and it's the same five extra minutes as melting butter into flour either way. Drain the tuna hard, pressing it against the strainer, or the extra liquid waters down a sauce that's already thin enough to soak into the noodles.

Potato chips over breadcrumbs is the point: they toast instead of just browning, and they hold their crunch against the sauce longer. Assemble the casserole up to a day ahead and refrigerate, but don't add the chips until just before it goes in the oven, or they'll be soft by the time you get there.
