---
title: Stovetop Queso with Pickled Jalapeños
date: 2026-08-01 05:30:03 -0400
description: Melted cheese, canned tomatoes, and pickled heat — ready before the chips run out.
servings: 6
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [appetizer, dip, cheese, spicy, quick]
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp | butter
  - ½ | small white onion, finely diced
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 1 can (10 oz) | diced tomatoes with green chiles, drained
  - ¾ cup | heavy cream
  - 1 lb | block American cheese, cubed
  - 1 cup | sharp cheddar, shredded
  - ¼ cup | pickled jalapeños, chopped, plus more to finish
  - To finish | cilantro, chopped
  - To taste | salt
method:
  - Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and cook the onion until it softens and turns translucent, about 4 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Stir in the tomatoes and cream and bring to a bare simmer.
  - Reduce the heat to low and add the American cheese a handful at a time, stirring until each addition melts before adding more.
  - Stir in the cheddar in the same way, until the whole pot is glossy and pulls together into one sauce.
  - Fold in the chopped jalapeños and season with salt.
  - Transfer to a serving bowl, top with more jalapeños and cilantro, and serve warm with tortilla chips.
---

The American cheese is doing structural work, not just flavor — it melts smooth and stays that way as it cools, where an all-cheddar queso breaks into a greasy puddle within ten minutes of leaving the stove. Keep the cheddar for the sharpness and let the American carry the texture.

Reheats fine on the stove with a splash more cream if it's gone stiff, but it's built to be eaten fast, straight out of the pot it was made in.
