---
title: Classic Buttermilk Coleslaw with Celery Seed
date: 2026-08-02 12:00:03 -0400
description: Salted and drained before it's dressed, so the cabbage stays crisp instead of turning the bowl watery.
image: /assets/images/Buttermilk-Coleslaw.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 1 hr chill
tags: [side, vegetarian, quick, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - ½ head | green cabbage, shredded (about 6 cups)
  - 2 | carrots, grated
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ cup | mayonnaise
  - 2 tbsp | buttermilk
  - 1 tbsp | apple cider vinegar
  - 1 tsp | celery seed
  - 1 tsp | sugar
  - ½ tsp | Dijon mustard
  - To taste | black pepper
method:
  - Toss the cabbage and carrots with the salt in a colander set over a bowl, and let them sit for 20 minutes until wilted, with a pool of liquid collected underneath.
  - Press the cabbage firmly against the colander to force out any remaining liquid, then pat dry with a towel.
  - Whisk the mayonnaise, buttermilk, vinegar, celery seed, sugar, and mustard together until smooth.
  - Toss the drained cabbage and carrots with the dressing until every strand is coated.
  - Cover and chill for at least 1 hour, until the dressing has thickened slightly and the cabbage has softened just past raw.
---

The salting step is the difference between coleslaw and a bowl of cabbage soup. Skip it and the salt and sugar in the dressing pull water out of the cabbage anyway — it just happens in the serving bowl instead of the colander, thinning the dressing and pooling at the bottom.

This is the creamy style, not the vinegar-forward kind — pile it on pulled pork or fried chicken where you want something cool and rich against the heat, rather than another layer of sharpness.
