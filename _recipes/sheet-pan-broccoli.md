title: Crispy Sheet-Pan Broccoli with Garlic and Parmesan

description: Broccoli roasted hot and fast on a preheated pan until the edges char, then finished with garlic, parmesan, and lemon.
image: /assets/images/Crispy-Sheet-Pan-Broccoli-With-Garlic-And-Parmesan.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 18 min
tags: [side, vegetarian, quick, roasted]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | broccoli, cut into florets
  - 3 tbsp | olive oil
  - 4 cloves | garlic, minced
  - ½ cup | grated parmesan
  - ½ | lemon
  - Salt and pepper, to taste
method:
  - Heat oven to 425°F with a rimmed sheet pan inside to preheat.
  - Toss the broccoli florets with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  - Carefully spread the broccoli in a single layer on the hot pan, cut sides down.
  - Roast until the edges are deeply browned and the stalks are tender when pierced with a knife, 15 to 18 minutes.
  - Scatter the parmesan over the broccoli and return to the oven until melted, about 2 minutes.
  - Squeeze the lemon over the top and serve immediately.
---

The preheated pan is the whole trick — broccoli dropped onto hot metal starts browning on contact instead of steaming in its own moisture for the first five minutes. Don't skip it, and don't crowd the pan either; florets need space between them or they'll steam each other soft.

Swap in cauliflower florets for half the broccoli if you want the mix, or add a pinch of red pepper flakes with the garlic for heat. Either way, get it to the table hot — it goes soft and sad fast once it sits.
