---
title: Poached Chicken Salad with Grapes and Tarragon
date: 2026-08-02 18:00:03 -0400
description: Cold poached chicken folded into a mayo-Dijon dressing with grapes, celery, and tarragon — no stove needed once it's in the fridge.
image: /assets/images/Chicken-Salad-With-Tarragon.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [chicken, salad, quick, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1½ lb | boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  - 1 | small onion, quartered
  - 2 | bay leaves
  - ¾ cup | mayonnaise
  - 1 tbsp | Dijon mustard
  - ½ | lemon, juiced
  - 2 stalks | celery, diced
  - 1 cup | red grapes, halved
  - ¼ cup | pecans, toasted and chopped
  - 2 tbsp | fresh tarragon, chopped
  - To taste | salt and pepper
method:
  - Cover the chicken with cold water in a pot, add the onion, bay leaves, and a big pinch of salt, and bring to a bare simmer.
  - Poach until the chicken is no longer pink at the center, 12-15 minutes, then let it cool in the liquid for 10 minutes.
  - Lift out the chicken and shred or dice it into bite-size pieces.
  - Whisk the mayonnaise, Dijon, lemon juice, salt, and pepper together in a large bowl.
  - Fold in the chicken, celery, grapes, pecans, and tarragon until every piece is coated.
  - Chill at least 1 hour before serving so the flavors settle.
---

Poaching the chicken whole in barely-simmering water, then letting it cool in that liquid, is what keeps it from turning stringy — a hard boil pushes moisture out exactly where a cold salad needs it most. Do this step a day ahead and the rest is five minutes of folding.

Tarragon is the one flavor here that isn't optional; its faint anise note is what separates this from a plain deli scoop. Swap the grapes for diced apple in fall, or pile the whole thing onto a croissant instead of bread.
