title: Bacon and Cheddar Baked Egg Muffins
date: 2026-08-12 09:00:03 -0400
description: Whisked eggs baked in a muffin tin with crisp bacon and sharp cheddar — grab-and-go breakfast that reheats in a minute flat.
image: /assets/images/Bacon-Cheddar-Egg-Muffins.webp
servings: 12
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [breakfast, eggs, quick, make-ahead, gluten-free, high-protein]
ingredients:
  - 6 slices | bacon
  - 10 | eggs
  - ¼ cup | whole milk
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - ¼ tsp | black pepper
  - 1 cup | sharp cheddar, shredded
  - 3 | scallions, thinly sliced
  - Nonstick spray | for the tin
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 350°F and cook the bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crisp, 6 to 8 minutes, then drain on paper towels and chop.
  - Spray a 12-cup muffin tin heavily, including the rims — eggs stick harder than batter does.
  - Whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper together until no streaks of white remain.
  - Divide the bacon, cheddar, and scallions among the cups, then pour the egg mixture over, filling each about three-quarters full.
  - Bake until the muffins are puffed, set in the center, and just barely golden at the edges, 16 to 18 minutes.
  - Let cool in the tin for 5 minutes — they'll deflate slightly as they cool, which is normal — then run a knife around each one to release.
---

The eggs puff up dramatically in the oven and sink back down as they cool; that's air escaping, not a sign of overbaking, so don't chase the dome by leaving them in longer. Pull them the moment the centers stop looking wet.

These are built for the fridge: cool completely, store in an airtight container up to 4 days, and microwave two at a time from cold for about 45 seconds. Swap the bacon and cheddar for whatever needs using up — diced ham, crumbled sausage, or sautéed spinach and feta all fold in the same way.
