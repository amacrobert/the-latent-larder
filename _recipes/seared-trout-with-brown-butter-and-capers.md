---
title: Seared Trout with Brown Butter and Capers
date: 2026-07-25 22:10:48 -0400
description: Ten minutes from pan to plate, and the browned butter does all the work of a sauce.
image: /assets/images/Seared-Trout-With-Brown-Butter-And-Capers.webp
servings: 2
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 8 min
tags: [fish, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 2 fillets | trout, skin-on (about 6 oz each)
  - To taste | Salt and pepper
  - 2 tbsp | olive oil
  - 4 tbsp | butter
  - 2 tbsp | capers, drained
  - 1 | lemon, juiced
  - A handful | parsley, chopped
method:
  - Pat the trout fillets dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  - Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers.
  - Lay the fillets skin-side down and press gently for 10 seconds so the skin doesn't curl.
  - Cook undisturbed until the skin is crisp and lifts cleanly from the pan, about 4 minutes.
  - Flip and cook until the flesh is opaque and flakes with a fork, about 2 minutes more.
  - Transfer the fillets to a plate and wipe out the skillet.
  - Melt the butter over medium heat until the foam subsides and it turns golden-brown and smells nutty.
  - Stir in the capers and lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits, then pull the pan off the heat.
  - Pour the butter over the trout and scatter with parsley.
---

Watch the butter, not the clock. It goes from foaming to brown in about a minute, and from brown to burnt almost as fast — pull it the moment it smells nutty and looks like light hazelnut skin. The capers stop the cooking and add the salt-and-acid hit that a rich fish needs.

Any thin white fish or a salmon fillet works the same way. Serve it over something that can soak up the butter — boiled potatoes, wilted greens, or just bread.
