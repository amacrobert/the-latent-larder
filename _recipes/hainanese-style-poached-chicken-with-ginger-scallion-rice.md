title: Hainanese-Style Poached Chicken with Ginger-Scallion Rice
date: 2026-07-31 06:30:03 -0400
description: A whole chicken barely simmered until just cooked, served warm over rice steamed in its own poaching stock — the ginger-scallion sauce is not optional.
image: /assets/images/Hainanese-Style-Poached-Chicken-With-Ginger-Scallion-Rice.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 1 hr
tags: [chicken, rice, slow, main, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1 (3½ lb) | whole chicken
  - 4 slices | ginger
  - 4 | scallions, smashed
  - 2 tbsp | Shaoxing wine
  - 1 tbsp | kosher salt
  - 2 cups | jasmine rice
  - 2 tbsp | neutral oil
  - 2 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 3 tbsp | ginger, minced
  - 3 | scallions, minced
  - 3 tbsp | neutral oil, for sauce
  - To finish | cucumber slices and cilantro
  - To taste | soy sauce, for serving
---

Submerge the chicken in a pot with the smashed ginger, smashed scallions, Shaoxing wine, and salt, and bring to a bare simmer. Poach gently, skimming any foam, until a thigh reads 165°F, keeping the water just below a boil so the skin stays smooth instead of tearing. Lift the chicken out and plunge it into a bowl of ice water until fully cooled, then strain and reserve the poaching liquid.

Heat the neutral oil in a pot with the minced garlic until fragrant, stir in the rice to coat, then cook it in 2 cups of the reserved poaching liquid until the grains are tender and the liquid is absorbed. Combine the minced ginger and minced scallions in a heatproof bowl; heat the remaining oil until shimmering and pour it over the top, where it should sizzle into a loose, fragrant sauce. Carve the chicken through the bone, arrange it over the rice, and spoon the ginger-scallion sauce on top, with cucumber, cilantro, and soy sauce alongside.

The ice bath is what gives the skin its jelly-like snap — skip it and the chicken just tastes boiled. No whole chicken on hand, bone-in thighs work, though shave fifteen minutes off the poaching time.
