---
title: Fresh Cranberry Sauce with Orange and Ginger
date: 2026-08-11 09:00:02 -0400
description: Whole cranberries simmered down with orange zest and fresh ginger until they burst — the one holiday side that takes fifteen minutes and improves for sitting.
image: /assets/images/Fresh-Cranberry-Sauce-With-Orange-And-Ginger.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [side, fruit, vegetarian, gluten-free, make-ahead, quick]
ingredients:
  - 12 oz | fresh cranberries
  - ¾ cup | granulated sugar
  - ½ cup | orange juice
  - 1 tbsp | orange zest
  - 1 tbsp | fresh ginger, grated
  - A pinch | kosher salt
method:
  - Combine the cranberries, sugar, orange juice, orange zest, ginger, and salt in a saucepan over medium heat.
  - Bring to a simmer, stirring occasionally to dissolve the sugar.
  - Cook until most of the berries have burst and the liquid has thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon, 12 to 15 minutes.
  - Remove from heat and cool to room temperature — it thickens further as it sits.
  - Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.
---

Don't cook it past the point where the berries burst on their own — a hard boil turns the sauce gluey instead of loose and jammy. The sauce is at its best made a day or two ahead; the ginger mellows and the whole thing sets up into slices instead of a puddle.

Swap the orange juice for apple cider if you want it less sharp, and leave the ginger out entirely for the plain version — everything else about the ratio holds.
