---
title: Farro Salad with Peaches, Arugula, and Pecorino
date: 2026-07-26 15:45:49 -0400
description: A boiled-grain salad built for the peak of peach season, dressed while everything's still warm enough to drink in the vinaigrette.
image: /assets/images/Farro-Salad-With-Peaches-Arugula-And-Pecorino.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [salad, vegetarian, quick, side, fruit]
ingredients:
  - 1 cup | farro, uncooked
  - 3 cups | water
  - 1 | shallot, thinly sliced
  - ¼ cup | olive oil
  - 2 tbsp | red wine vinegar
  - 2 | peaches, sliced
  - 2 cups | arugula
  - ½ cup | pecorino, shaved
  - To finish | flaky salt and black pepper
method:
  - Bring the water to a boil, add the farro, and simmer until tender but still chewy, about 20 minutes.
  - Drain and spread the farro on a sheet pan to cool slightly, about 5 minutes.
  - Whisk the olive oil and vinegar with a pinch of salt in a large bowl, then stir in the shallot and let it sit while the farro cools.
  - Add the warm farro to the bowl and toss to coat, then let it sit for 10 minutes to soak up the dressing.
  - Fold in the peaches and arugula.
  - Scatter the pecorino over the top and finish with flaky salt and pepper just before serving.
---

Dress the farro while it's still warm, not after it's gone cold on the pan — warm grain drinks the vinaigrette instead of just wearing it on the surface. The brief soak for the shallot does the same job a proper pickle would in a fraction of the time: it takes the raw edge off without turning it fully soft.

No peaches, use nectarines or a ripe plum, cut into wedges. It holds at room temperature for a couple of hours, so it's a good one to make ahead of a table that isn't ready the moment the food is.
