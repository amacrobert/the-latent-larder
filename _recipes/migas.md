---
title: Skillet Migas with Melted Monterey Jack
date: 2026-08-07 06:00:03 -0400
description: Scrambled eggs folded through crisped corn tortilla strips, blistered jalapeño, and melted cheese — built to use up tortillas that have gone a day stale.
image: /assets/images/Skillet-Migas-With-Melted-Monterey-Jack.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [breakfast, mexican, vegetarian, gluten-free, quick]
ingredients:
  - 6 | corn tortillas, cut into strips
  - 3 tbsp | vegetable oil, divided
  - 1 | small white onion, diced
  - 1 | jalapeño, diced
  - 1 | plum tomato, diced
  - 8 | large eggs, beaten
  - ½ cup | shredded Monterey Jack
  - To finish | cilantro, avocado, hot sauce
  - Salt and pepper, to taste
method:
  - Fry the tortilla strips in 2 tbsp oil over medium-high heat, stirring often, until stiff and golden at the edges, about 4 minutes, then drain on paper towels.
  - Add the remaining oil to the same skillet and cook the onion and jalapeño until the onion turns translucent and the jalapeño skin blisters in spots.
  - Stir in the tomato and cook just until it softens and releases its juice, about 1 minute.
  - Return the tortilla strips to the skillet and toss to coat them in the vegetables.
  - Pour in the beaten eggs, season with salt and pepper, and fold slowly over medium-low heat until just set but still glossy, about 3 minutes.
  - Scatter the cheese over top, cover, and let it melt off the heat for a minute.
  - Finish with cilantro, avocado, and hot sauce, and serve right away.
---

The strips are the whole point, and they only work if they still have some bite left when the eggs hit the pan — fry them until the edges curl and stiffen, not until they're fully crackery, or they'll turn to mush the second they meet the egg. Day-old tortillas fry up crisper than fresh ones, so this is the right move for a bag that's starting to dry out.

Fold the eggs slowly and pull them off the heat while they still look a touch underdone; they'll finish setting from residual heat, and overcooked eggs are the fastest way to make the strips soggy by the time you serve. Leftover strips wrapped in warm flour tortillas make a fast migas taco the next morning.
