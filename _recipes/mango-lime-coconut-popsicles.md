---
title: Mango-Lime Coconut Popsicles
date: 2026-08-06 18:00:03 -0400
description: Blended, poured into molds, and frozen solid — the only cooking required is none at all.
image: /assets/images/Mango-Lime-Coconut-Popsicles.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 6 hrs (freezing)
tags: [dessert, fruit, vegetarian, gluten-free, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 3 cups | mango, frozen or fresh, cubed
  - 1 can | full-fat coconut milk (13.5 oz)
  - 3 tbsp | lime juice
  - 3 tbsp | honey
  - Pinch | salt
method:
  - Blend the mango, coconut milk, lime juice, honey, and salt until completely smooth, scraping down the sides once.
  - Taste and add more honey if the mango is underripe — the mix should taste slightly too sweet, since freezing dulls it.
  - Divide among popsicle molds, tapping each one on the counter to knock out air bubbles.
  - Insert sticks and freeze until solid all the way through, at least 6 hours or overnight.
  - To unmold, run the outside of each mold under warm water for a few seconds until the pop releases.
---

Full-fat coconut milk is what keeps these from freezing into rock-hard ice —
the fat stays slightly pliable at freezer temperature, so a popsicle scraped
straight from the tray is still soft enough to bite into instead of licking
until it wears down. Light coconut milk or a splash of water instead will
freeze harder and icier.

Ripeness does the flavor work here, not technique, so use mango soft enough
to dent under a thumb. Frozen mango chunks work fine and skip the peeling —
just blend a minute longer since the mix starts colder and thicker.
