---
title: Grapefruit Paloma with Chili-Salt Rim
date: 2026-07-30 07:00:02 -0400
description: Tequila, lime, and grapefruit soda over ice, rimmed with chili salt so the first sip turns from hot to bitter to sweet before it's gone.
image: /assets/images/Grapefruit-Paloma-With-Chili-Salt-Rim.webp
servings: 1
prep_time: 5 min
tags: [drink, quick, spicy, fruit]
ingredients:
  - 1 wedge | lime, plus more for garnish
  - 1 tbsp | chili salt
  - 2 oz | blanco tequila
  - ¾ oz | lime juice
  - 4 oz | grapefruit soda
  - To fill | ice
method:
  - Run the lime wedge around the rim of a rocks glass, then press the rim into the chili salt until it's coated on one side.
  - Fill the glass with ice, taking care not to knock the salted rim loose.
  - Pour in the tequila and lime juice.
  - Top with the grapefruit soda and give it one short stir, just enough to combine without beating the fizz out of it.
  - Garnish with a lime wheel and drink it while the soda's still lively.
---

Grapefruit soda is doing more work here than fresh juice ever could — it's already sweetened and carbonated, so the drink stays balanced without a syrup and keeps its fizz through the last sip instead of going flat and sour like a fresh-squeezed version would. The chili salt is the other half of the drink: hit it first and the sip reads hot, then bitter, then sweet, in that order, every time.

No chili salt on hand, mix flaky salt with a pinch of cayenne or chili powder — the ratio isn't precise, just enough red to see. For a pitcher version, batch the tequila and lime juice ahead in the fridge and add the soda and ice glass by glass, since the fizz doesn't hold once it's mixed.
