title: Oven-Baked Baby Back Ribs with Brown Sugar Rub
date: 2026-08-02 08:00:03 -0400
description: Low oven first to render the fat until it falls off the bone, then a broiler finish under a sticky glaze.
image: /assets/images/Oven-Baked-Baby-Back-Ribs.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 3 hr 15 min
tags: [pork, baking, slow, main, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2 racks | baby back ribs (about 2 lb each), membrane removed
  - ¼ cup | brown sugar
  - 1 tbsp | smoked paprika
  - 1 tbsp | kosher salt
  - 2 tsp | garlic powder
  - 2 tsp | onion powder
  - 1 tsp | black pepper
  - ½ tsp | cayenne
  - 1 cup | ketchup
  - 2 tbsp | apple cider vinegar
  - 2 tbsp | brown sugar
  - 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce
  - 1 clove | garlic, minced
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 300°F.
  - Stir together the brown sugar, smoked paprika, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and cayenne.
  - Rub the mixture over both sides of each rack, pressing it into the meat.
  - Wrap each rack tightly in foil and set on a rimmed baking sheet, meat side up.
  - Bake until the meat has pulled back from the bone ends and a fork slides in with no resistance, about 2½ to 3 hours.
  - Meanwhile, simmer the ketchup, vinegar, remaining brown sugar, Worcestershire, and minced garlic in a small saucepan over medium-low heat until glossy and slightly thickened, about 10 minutes.
  - Unwrap the ribs and heat the broiler.
  - Brush both sides generously with the glaze and broil 4 to 5 inches from the heat until the glaze bubbles and catches char in spots, 3 to 5 minutes.
  - Rest 5 minutes, then slice between the bones.
---

The membrane on the back of the rack is the one step people skip and regret — left on, it turns leathery and keeps the rub from getting in at all. Slide a butter knife under it at one corner, grip it with a paper towel, and peel it off in one piece.

Foil during the long bake is what keeps the ribs from drying out before they're tender; save the open, dry heat for the glaze at the end so it tightens into something sticky instead of just melting off. Leftovers reheat well wrapped in foil at 300°F for 20 minutes.
