---
title: Tomato and Corn Galette
date: 2026-07-26 14:45:29 -0400
description: A free-form cornmeal crust folded around ripe tomatoes and corn, no pan or tart ring required.
image: /assets/images/Tomato-And-Corn-Galette.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 45 min
cook_time: 40 min
tags: [baking, vegetarian, main]
ingredients:
  - 1¼ cups | all-purpose flour
  - ¼ cup | cornmeal, plus more for dusting
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter, cold and cubed
  - ¼ cup | ice water
  - 3 | large tomatoes, sliced
  - 1 cup | corn kernels
  - ½ cup | shredded Gruyère
  - 1 | egg, beaten
  - To finish | flaky salt and basil
method:
  - Cut the cold butter into the flour, cornmeal, and salt until the biggest pieces are pea-sized.
  - Add the ice water a tablespoon at a time, tossing until the dough just clumps when you squeeze it.
  - Press the dough into a disk, wrap it, and chill until firm, at least 30 minutes.
  - Lay the tomato slices on paper towels and salt them while the dough chills, so the crust doesn't drown.
  - Roll the dough into a rough 12-inch round on parchment.
  - Scatter a layer of cornmeal over the center, leaving a 2-inch border, then layer on the Gruyère, tomatoes, and corn.
  - Fold the border over the filling in overlapping pleats, working your way around, then brush the crust with egg.
  - Bake at 400°F until the crust is deep golden and the filling is bubbling at the edges, about 40 minutes.
  - Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing, then finish with flaky salt and torn basil.
---

The tomatoes will weep no matter what, which is why they get salted and drained first — skip that step and the bottom of the crust turns to paste. Keep the butter cold going into the oven too; that's what gives you flakes instead of a cracker.

Out of season, zucchini or roasted peppers work in place of the corn and tomatoes. Good warm or at room temperature, with nothing more than a green salad next to it.
