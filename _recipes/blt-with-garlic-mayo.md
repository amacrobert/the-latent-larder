---
title: BLT with Thick-Cut Bacon and Garlic Mayo
date: 2026-08-01 03:00:03 -0400
description: Six strips of bacon fried hard, cold lettuce, salted tomato, and a garlic mayo that earns the sandwich its name.
servings: 2
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [pork, sandwich, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 6 slices | thick-cut bacon
  - 4 slices | sturdy white or sourdough bread
  - 1 large | tomato, sliced ½-inch thick
  - Salt | for the tomato
  - 4 leaves | romaine or iceberg lettuce
  - ½ cup | mayonnaise
  - 1 clove | garlic, grated
  - To taste | black pepper
method:
  - Lay the bacon in a cold skillet, then turn the heat to medium and cook, flipping occasionally, until deeply browned and crisp at the edges, 8 to 10 minutes.
  - Drain the bacon on paper towels, leaving the fat in the pan.
  - Salt the tomato slices on both sides and let them sit on paper towels while the bacon cooks, to pull out excess water.
  - Stir the grated garlic into the mayonnaise and season with black pepper.
  - Toast the bread, then spread garlic mayo on every slice, edge to edge.
  - Layer lettuce, tomato, and bacon between two mayo-covered slices per sandwich, pressing down slightly before you cut.
  - Cut on the diagonal and eat immediately, before the toast softens.
---

The tomato is the part people skip and shouldn't — salting it for even five minutes while the bacon renders keeps the bread from going soggy by serving time. Cold-starting the bacon in the pan renders more fat before it browns, which is what gets it flat and crisp instead of curled and chewy.

Swap the mayo base for a mashed avocado if you want richness without the pure fat, though the garlic still goes in either way. Leftover bacon fat is worth keeping — a spoonful stirred into the mayo instead of grated garlic makes a smokier version of the same sandwich.
