---
title: Crispy Cannoli with Sweet Ricotta and Pistachio
date: 2026-08-13 03:00:03 -0400
description: Fried pastry tubes filled with sweetened ricotta at the last minute, so the shells stay glass-crisp instead of going soft.
image: /assets/images/Cannoli.webp
servings: 12
prep_time: 1 hr 30 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [dessert, fried, vegetarian, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2 cups | ricotta, drained overnight
  - ¾ cup | powdered sugar
  - 1 tsp | vanilla extract
  - ½ cup | mini chocolate chips
  - 2 cups | flour
  - 2 tbsp | granulated sugar
  - ¼ tsp | ground cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp | salt
  - 3 tbsp | cold butter, cubed
  - 1 | egg, separated
  - 6 tbsp | dry Marsala wine
  - 1 tbsp | white wine vinegar
  - 4 cups | neutral oil, for frying
  - To finish | chopped pistachios and powdered sugar
method:
  - Whisk the flour, granulated sugar, cinnamon, and salt together, then cut in the cold butter with your fingers until the mixture looks like coarse sand.
  - Stir in the egg yolk, Marsala, and vinegar with a fork until a shaggy dough forms, then knead on a floured counter until smooth, about 5 minutes. Wrap and refrigerate 1 hour.
  - Roll the dough as thin as it will go without tearing — a pasta roller on the second-to-last setting works well — and cut into 4-inch circles.
  - Wrap each circle around a metal cannoli tube, overlapping the edge slightly, and seal the seam with a dab of the egg white.
  - Heat 2 inches of oil to 350°F in a heavy pot. Fry the tubes 3 or 4 at a time, turning once, until deep golden and blistered, about 2 minutes.
  - Lift onto a paper-towel-lined rack with tongs. Once cool enough to handle, twist the shells off the tubes and let cool completely.
  - Beat the ricotta, powdered sugar, and vanilla until smooth, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  - Pipe the filling into both ends of each shell just before serving. Dip the filled ends in pistachios and dust with powdered sugar.
---

Fill these at the last minute, not ahead — ricotta is mostly water even after draining, and a filled shell goes from crisp to soggy inside an hour. Fry the shells a day or two early instead and keep them in an airtight container at room temperature; the filling takes ten minutes to whip up right before serving.

No metal cannoli tubes? Wrap 1-inch wooden dowels tightly in foil and use those — the shells release just as easily. Skip the Marsala and use dry white wine plus an extra teaspoon of vinegar if that's what's open.
