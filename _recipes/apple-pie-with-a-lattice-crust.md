---
title: Apple Pie with a Lattice Crust
date: 2026-07-29 22:30:03 -0400
description: A double crust built from cold butter and a short chill, holding in a mound of macerated apples until the lattice bubbles over.
image: /assets/images/Apple-Pie-With-A-Lattice-Crust.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 40 min
cook_time: 55 min
tags: [dessert, baking, fruit, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2½ cups | all-purpose flour
  - 1 tsp | salt
  - 2 tbsp | sugar
  - 1 cup | cold butter, cubed
  - 6 tbsp | ice water, more as needed
  - 3 lb | apples, peeled and sliced
  - ¾ cup | sugar
  - ¼ cup | all-purpose flour
  - 1 tsp | cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp | nutmeg
  - 1 | lemon, juiced
  - 2 tbsp | butter, cubed
  - To finish | egg wash and coarse sugar
method:
  - Pulse the flour, salt, and sugar, then cut in the cold butter until the largest pieces are pea-sized.
  - Drizzle in the ice water, tossing just until the dough clumps when pressed between your fingers.
  - Divide the dough in two, pat into disks, wrap, and chill at least 1 hour, until firm.
  - Toss the apples with the sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice until every slice is coated and sitting in its own juice.
  - Roll one disk into a 12-inch round and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate, trimming the overhang to an inch.
  - Pile in the apples, mounding them at the center, and scatter the butter over the top.
  - Roll the second disk, cut it into strips, and weave them into a lattice over the filling.
  - Crimp the edges, brush the lattice with egg wash, sprinkle with coarse sugar, and chill 15 minutes to firm the butter back up.
  - Bake at 425°F for 20 minutes, then lower the oven to 375°F and continue until the juices bubble thick through the lattice and the crust is deep gold, about 35 minutes more.
  - Cool on a rack at least 3 hours before slicing, so the filling has time to set instead of running.
---

Cold butter is the whole trick — if it softens before it hits the oven, the crust bakes flat instead of flaky. Work fast, and if the kitchen's warm, chill the rolled-out bottom crust for ten minutes before filling it.

Mix a tart apple like Granny Smith with a sweeter one like Honeycrisp; all of one or the other makes a filling that's either sour enough to plaster or slack enough to weep. Serve it the way it always gets served, with vanilla ice cream going soft against a warm slice.
