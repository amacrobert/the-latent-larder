---
title: Cranberry-Orange Scones with Turbinado Sugar
date: 2026-08-05 19:00:03 -0400
description: Wedge-cut cream scones studded with dried cranberries and orange zest, sturdy enough to hold their shape and craggy on top.
image: /assets/images/Cranberry-Orange-Scones-With-Turbinado-Sugar.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 20 min
cook_time: 20 min
tags: [breakfast, baking, vegetarian, fruit, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2½ cups | all-purpose flour
  - 6 tbsp | sugar
  - 1 tbsp | baking powder
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - ½ cup | cold unsalted butter, cubed
  - ¾ cup | dried cranberries
  - 1 | orange, zested
  - 1 cup | heavy cream, plus more for brushing
  - 1 | egg
  - To finish | turbinado sugar
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  - Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  - Cut the cold butter into the flour with your fingers or a pastry cutter until the largest pieces are pea-sized.
  - Stir in the cranberries and orange zest.
  - Whisk the cream and egg together, then pour into the dry ingredients and stir just until a shaggy dough comes together.
  - Turn the dough onto a floured surface and pat into an 8-inch round about 1 inch thick.
  - Cut into 8 wedges and transfer to the baking sheet, spacing them an inch apart.
  - Brush the tops with cream and sprinkle heavily with turbinado sugar.
  - Bake until the tops are golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean, 18 to 20 minutes.
  - Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
---

The dough should still look a little shaggy and butter-streaked when you pat it out — overworking it here is what turns a craggy, tender scone into a dense one. Keep the butter cold going in; if it's softened by the time you're cutting wedges, slide the whole tray into the freezer for 10 minutes before it hits the oven.

Swap the cranberries for chopped dried apricot or a handful of chocolate chips in the same amount, and skip the zest if you're leaving out the orange altogether. These freeze well unbaked — cut the wedges, freeze solid on the tray, then bake straight from frozen with an extra 3 to 4 minutes in the oven.
