---
title: Baked Buffalo Chicken Dip
date: 2026-08-02 06:00:03 -0400
description: Shredded chicken and cream cheese baked hot with enough Frank's to bite back — the dip that empties fastest at any table with a game on.
image: /assets/images/Baked-Buffalo-Chicken-Dip.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [chicken, appetizer, dip, spicy, baking, cheese, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2 cups | shredded cooked chicken
  - 8 oz | cream cheese, softened
  - ½ cup | ranch dressing
  - ½ cup | hot sauce, such as Frank's
  - 1 cup | shredded sharp cheddar
  - ½ cup | crumbled blue cheese
  - 2 | scallions, thinly sliced
  - To serve | celery sticks and tortilla chips
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  - Beat the cream cheese in a bowl until smooth, then stir in the ranch dressing and hot sauce until no streaks of either remain.
  - Fold in the shredded chicken along with half the cheddar and half the blue cheese.
  - Spread the mixture into a small baking dish and scatter the remaining cheddar and blue cheese over the top.
  - Bake until the edges bubble and the top has browned in spots, 20 to 25 minutes.
  - Scatter the scallions over the dip and serve hot with celery sticks and tortilla chips.
---

Rotisserie chicken is the whole point here — shred it while it's still warm and skip poaching a breast just for this. The ranch is doing real work, not just filler: it mellows the hot sauce enough that the dip stays spoonable instead of separating into an oil slick.

Assemble it a day ahead and hold it covered in the fridge; bake straight from cold, adding five minutes to the time. Leftovers reheat fine and make an unreasonably good grilled cheese.
