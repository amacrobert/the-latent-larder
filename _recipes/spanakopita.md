---
title: Spanakopita
date: 2026-07-28 03:46:03 -0400
description: Spinach and feta packed between layers of butter-brushed phyllo, baked until the top shatters like glass.
image: /assets/images/Spanakopita.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 30 min
cook_time: 45 min
tags: [vegetarian, baking, main]
ingredients:
  - 2 lb | fresh spinach
  - 1 | yellow onion, diced
  - 4 | scallions, sliced
  - 3 cloves | garlic, minced
  - 3 tbsp | olive oil
  - 8 oz | feta, crumbled
  - 4 oz | ricotta
  - 3 | eggs, beaten
  - 2 tbsp | fresh dill, chopped
  - 2 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped
  - ½ tsp | ground nutmeg
  - To taste | salt and black pepper
  - 1 lb | phyllo dough, thawed
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter, melted
method:
  - Wilt the spinach in batches in a hot, dry pan until it collapses down to almost nothing.
  - Cool the spinach, then squeeze it dry in a kitchen towel until no more liquid runs out.
  - Sweat the onion and scallions in the olive oil until soft and translucent.
  - Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  - Chop the squeezed spinach and combine with the onion mixture, feta, ricotta, eggs, dill, parsley, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
  - Brush a baking dish with melted butter, then layer in half the phyllo sheets, brushing each one with butter before adding the next.
  - Spread the spinach filling evenly over the phyllo.
  - Layer the remaining phyllo sheets on top, brushing each with butter, then tuck the overhanging edges down into the dish.
  - Score the top layers into portions with a sharp knife so the crust doesn't shatter unevenly later.
  - Bake at 375°F until deep golden and crisp, about 45 minutes.
  - Let it rest at least 15 minutes before cutting so the filling sets.
---

Squeeze the spinach harder than feels necessary — any water left behind turns the filling watery under all that pastry, and there's no fixing it once it's baked. A towel twisted tight is more reliable than pressing it in a colander.

Phyllo dries out and cracks the moment it's exposed to air, so keep the stack under a damp towel while you work and only peel off sheets as you need them. This keeps well at room temperature for hours, which makes it as much a party dish as a dinner one — cut it small for a spread, or in wedges alongside a green salad for a light main.
