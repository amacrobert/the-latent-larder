title: Ground Beef Burrito Bowls with Cilantro-Lime Rice
date: 2026-08-09 12:00:02 -0400
description: Skillet taco beef, lime-cilantro rice, and all the fixings, built to portion into containers for the week.
image: /assets/images/Ground-Beef-Burrito-Bowls-With-Cilantro-Lime-Rice.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 25 min
tags: [beef, mexican, rice, quick, main, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1½ cups | long-grain rice
  - 2¼ cups | chicken broth
  - 2 | limes, zested and juiced, divided
  - A handful | cilantro, chopped, divided
  - 1 lb | ground beef, 80/20
  - 2 tsp | chili powder
  - 1 tsp | ground cumin
  - 1 tsp | garlic powder
  - ½ tsp | smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp | dried oregano
  - 1 can (15 oz) | black beans, drained and rinsed
  - 1 cup | corn kernels
  - 1 cup | cherry tomatoes, halved
  - ¼ cup | red onion, diced
  - 1 | avocado, sliced
  - 1 cup | shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack
  - To finish | sour cream and lime wedges
method:
  - Combine the rice and broth in a pot. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer over low heat until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender, about 18 minutes. Let it sit covered off the heat for 5 minutes, then fluff and stir in half the lime zest and juice and half the cilantro.
  - While the rice cooks, brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it goes, until no pink remains, about 6 minutes. Drain off excess fat.
  - Stir the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, paprika, and oregano into the beef along with ¼ cup water. Simmer until the liquid has mostly cooked off and the beef looks glossy, 3 to 4 minutes.
  - Wipe out the skillet and set it over high heat. Add the corn in a single layer and cook undisturbed until charred in spots, about 4 minutes.
  - Warm the black beans in a small pot or the microwave.
  - Divide the rice among bowls and top with the beef, black beans, charred corn, tomatoes, red onion, and avocado. Scatter the cheese over top while everything is still warm enough to soften it.
  - Finish with the remaining lime zest, juice, and cilantro, plus sour cream and lime wedges.
---

The beef only needs a splash of water and a few minutes to go from browned-and-dry to a glossy, cling-to-the-spoon filling — resist the urge to add more liquid or you'll end up simmering taco soup instead. Char the corn in the same skillet you browned the beef in; the fond left behind does more for the corn than any seasoning would.

These keep for four days in the fridge if you pack the avocado and sour cream separately and add them at serving time. Swap the beef for shredded rotisserie chicken or a second can of beans if you want to rotate proteins through the week without changing anything else.
