---
title: Nectarine and Almond Galette
date: 2026-07-26
description: A free-form pastry crust folded around sliced nectarines and almond meal, forgiving of a less-than-perfect fold.
image: /assets/images/Nectarine-And-Almond-Galette.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 30 min
cook_time: 45 min
tags: [dessert, baking, fruit]
ingredients:
  - 1¼ cups | all-purpose flour
  - ¼ tsp | salt
  - ½ cup | cold butter, cubed
  - 3-4 tbsp | ice water
  - ¼ cup | almond meal
  - 4 | nectarines, sliced
  - ¼ cup | sugar
  - 1 tbsp | cornstarch
  - 1 tbsp | lemon juice
  - 1 | egg, beaten
  - 1 tbsp | turbinado sugar
method:
  - Cut the cold butter into the flour and salt until the biggest pieces are pea-sized.
  - Add the ice water a tablespoon at a time, tossing until the dough just clumps when you squeeze it.
  - Press the dough into a disk, wrap it, and chill until firm, at least 30 minutes.
  - Toss the sliced nectarines with the sugar, cornstarch, and lemon juice.
  - Roll the chilled dough into a rough 12-inch round on parchment.
  - Scatter the almond meal over the center, leaving a 2-inch border, then arrange the nectarines on top.
  - Fold the border over the fruit in overlapping pleats, working your way around.
  - Brush the crust with egg and scatter it with turbinado sugar.
  - Bake at 400°F until the crust is deep golden and the juices are bubbling thickly, 40-45 minutes.
  - Let it cool at least 20 minutes before slicing, so the filling sets instead of running.
---

The almond meal isn't decoration — it sits under the fruit and soaks up the juice that would otherwise turn the crust to paste. Don't skip it, and don't skimp on the chill either; warm dough won't hold a pleat.

Plums or peaches swap in at the same weight when nectarines aren't at their best. Good slightly warm, with a scoop of vanilla ice cream melting into the folds.
