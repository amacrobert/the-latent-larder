---
title: Instant Pot Mac and Cheese with Sharp Cheddar
date: 2026-08-08 09:00:02 -0400
description: No roux, no drained pot — the pasta cooks in broth and thickens its own sauce under pressure in under ten minutes.
image: /assets/images/Instant-Pot-Mac-And-Cheese.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 4 min
tags: [pasta, cheese, quick, main, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | elbow macaroni
  - 4 cups | chicken broth
  - 4 tbsp | butter
  - 1 tsp | dry mustard powder
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - 12 oz | evaporated milk (1 can)
  - 4 cups | sharp cheddar, shredded off the block
  - 1 cup | Monterey Jack, shredded
  - To taste | black pepper
method:
  - Add the macaroni, broth, butter, mustard powder, and salt to the Instant Pot and stir so the pasta is fully submerged.
  - Seal the lid and cook on high pressure for 4 minutes.
  - Quick-release the pressure the moment the timer ends, so the pasta stops cooking before it turns to mush in the residual heat.
  - Stir in the evaporated milk, then add the cheeses a handful at a time, stirring until each addition melts before the next.
  - Keep stirring off heat until the sauce turns glossy and coats the back of a spoon — it thickens fast as it sits.
  - Season with pepper and serve right away, while the sauce is loosest.
---

Skipping the stovetop roux isn't a shortcut so much as the point: the macaroni releases enough starch cooking directly in the broth that the sauce thickens on its own once the cheese goes in. Evaporated milk does the enrichment a roux would otherwise handle — it's shelf-stable and low-moisture enough not to break under the residual heat the way fresh cream can. Quick-releasing immediately matters more here than in most pressure-cooker recipes, since the pasta keeps cooking in the trapped steam if you let it sit.

Monterey Jack melts smoother than a second helping of cheddar and keeps the sauce from turning grainy — swap in Gruyère for a nuttier version instead. This doesn't hold up as a make-ahead the way a baked version does; the sauce tightens considerably as it cools; loosen leftovers with a splash of milk over low heat rather than the microwave.
