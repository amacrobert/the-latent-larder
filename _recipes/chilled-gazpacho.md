---
title: Chilled Tomato Gazpacho with Cucumber
date: 2026-08-06 06:00:02 -0400
description: Raw tomatoes, cucumber, and peppers blended smooth and served cold — the only soup all summer that never touches a stove.
image: /assets/images/Chilled-Gazpacho.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 20 min (plus 2 hr chilling)
cook_time: 0 min
tags: [soup, vegetarian, gluten-free, quick, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 2 lb | ripe tomatoes, cored and quartered
  - 1 | English cucumber, peeled and chopped
  - 1 | red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  - 2 cloves | garlic
  - 2 tbsp | red wine vinegar
  - ¼ cup | good olive oil, plus more to finish
  - 2 slices | day-old bread, torn, crusts removed
  - To taste | salt
  - To finish | diced cucumber and tomato
method:
  - Combine the tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, garlic, vinegar, olive oil, torn bread, and 1 teaspoon salt in a blender.
  - Blend on high until completely smooth, 1-2 minutes, scraping down the sides once.
  - Taste and adjust with more vinegar or salt — it should taste a shade too sharp cold, since chilling mutes seasoning.
  - Pass through a fine-mesh strainer for a silkier texture, if you like, pressing on the solids to extract as much liquid as possible.
  - Cover and refrigerate until fully cold, at least 2 hours.
  - Ladle into bowls, top with diced cucumber and tomato, and finish with a drizzle of olive oil.
---

The torn bread isn't filler — it's what gives the soup body instead of leaving it as thin tomato water, a trick straight out of Andalusia where this started as a way to use up stale loaves. Skip it if you want something lighter, but the texture won't be the same.

This gets better after a night in the fridge, so make it the day before if you can. Serve it properly cold, in chilled bowls if you have them — a lukewarm gazpacho tastes like a mistake.
