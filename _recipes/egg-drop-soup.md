title: Silky Egg Drop Soup with Scallions
date: 2026-08-09 21:00:03 -0400
description: Chicken stock thickened just enough to hold beaten egg in ribbons — ten minutes from cold stove to bowl.
image: /assets/images/Silky-Egg-Drop-Soup-With-Scallions.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 5 min
cook_time: 10 min
tags: [soup, eggs, quick]
ingredients:
  - 4 cups | chicken stock
  - 2 tbsp | cornstarch
  - 3 tbsp | cold water
  - 3 | large eggs, beaten
  - 1 tsp | grated ginger
  - 1 tbsp | soy sauce
  - ½ tsp | white pepper
  - 1 tsp | toasted sesame oil
  - 2 | scallions, thinly sliced
  - To taste | salt
method:
  - Whisk the cornstarch into the cold water until smooth to make a slurry.
  - Bring the chicken stock, ginger, and soy sauce to a simmer in a saucepan.
  - Stir in the cornstarch slurry and simmer until the broth thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 1 minute.
  - Lower the heat until the broth barely moves, then drizzle in the beaten eggs in a thin, steady stream while stirring gently in one direction with a fork.
  - Let the egg sit undisturbed for about 10 seconds so it sets into ribbons, then stir once more to separate them.
  - Stir in the white pepper and sesame oil, then season with salt.
  - Ladle into bowls and top with scallions.
---

The two things that turn this into clumps instead of ribbons: a broth that's boiling too hard when the egg goes in, and stirring while you pour. Get the surface down to a bare shiver first, pour the egg in a thin stream from a height, and leave it alone until it sets before you touch it again.

Stir shredded rotisserie chicken and a handful of corn kernels into the finished broth for something closer to a meal than a starter, or swap in vegetable stock to make it meatless — the cornstarch does the same work either way.
