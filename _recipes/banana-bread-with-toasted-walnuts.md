---
title: Banana Bread with Toasted Walnuts
date: 2026-07-30 15:30:03 -0400
description: A dense, one-bowl loaf built to use up the bananas gone black on the counter.
image: /assets/images/Banana-Bread-With-Toasted-Walnuts.webp
servings: 8
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 1 hr
tags: [bread, baking, breakfast, vegetarian, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 3 | very ripe bananas
  - ½ cup | unsalted butter, melted
  - ¾ cup | sugar
  - 2 | eggs
  - 1 tsp | vanilla extract
  - 1¾ cups | all-purpose flour
  - 1 tsp | baking soda
  - ½ tsp | salt
  - 1 tsp | cinnamon
  - ¾ cup | walnuts, toasted and chopped
method:
  - Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  - Mash the bananas in a large bowl until mostly smooth, then whisk in the melted butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  - In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
  - Fold the dry ingredients into the wet just until no streaks of flour remain — a few lumps are fine, and overmixing is what makes a tough loaf.
  - Fold in the walnuts and scrape the batter into the pan, smoothing the top.
  - Bake until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean and the top is deeply browned and cracked down the middle, 55 to 65 minutes.
  - Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely before slicing.
---

The bananas are the whole recipe: they need to be fully black, soft enough to
mash with a fork, well past what looks appetizing on a counter. Underripe
bananas make a pale, barely sweet loaf no amount of sugar fixes.

Walnuts are the classic, but pecans work the same way, and a handful of
chocolate chips folded in alongside them doesn't hurt anyone. The loaf keeps
on the counter, wrapped, for several days, and only gets better once the
crumb settles overnight — good toasted with butter by the third day.
