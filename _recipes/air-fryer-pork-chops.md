---
title: Crispy Air Fryer Pork Chops with Garlic Butter
date: 2026-08-04 14:00:02 -0400
description: Boneless chops go crackling-brown in under fifteen minutes with no oil spatter, then get a pat of garlic butter that melts straight into the crust.
image: /assets/images/Air-Fryer-Pork-Chops.webp
servings: 4
prep_time: 10 min
cook_time: 15 min
tags: [pork, quick, main]
ingredients:
  - 4 | boneless pork chops, ¾-inch thick
  - 1 tbsp | olive oil
  - 1 tsp | garlic powder
  - 1 tsp | dried thyme
  - 1 tsp | kosher salt
  - ½ tsp | black pepper
  - 3 tbsp | butter, softened
  - 2 cloves | garlic, grated
  - 1 tbsp | chopped parsley
  - To finish | flaky salt
method:
  - Pat the chops dry, then toss with the oil, garlic powder, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  - Arrange in the air fryer basket in a single layer, without touching.
  - Cook at 400°F for 6 minutes.
  - Flip and cook until deep golden brown and the centers read 140°F, another 5 to 7 minutes.
  - While the chops cook, mash the butter with the grated garlic and parsley into a rough compound butter.
  - Rest the chops 5 minutes, then top each with a spoonful of the garlic butter and let it melt in before serving with flaky salt.
---

Boneless and ¾-inch is the sweet spot here — thick bone-in chops need longer than the crust can hold before drying out, and this cut hits 140°F right as the outside turns deep gold. Pull them the moment they get there; pork keeps climbing a few degrees as it rests.

The compound butter is doing what a pan sauce would do in a skillet recipe, just without the skillet — melted garlic and parsley pooling into every crack in the crust. Make a double batch and keep the extra in the fridge for steak or corn on the cob.
