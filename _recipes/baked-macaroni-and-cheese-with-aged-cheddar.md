---
title: Baked Macaroni and Cheese with Aged Cheddar
date: 2026-07-31 02:00:03 -0400
description: A real stovetop béchamel under a crust of buttered crumbs, not a bag of orange powder.
image: /assets/images/Baked-Macaroni-And-Cheese-With-Aged-Cheddar.webp
servings: 6
prep_time: 15 min
cook_time: 35 min
tags: [pasta, baking, main, vegetarian, cheese, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb | elbow macaroni
  - 6 tbsp | butter, divided
  - ¼ cup | flour
  - 3 cups | whole milk
  - 1 tsp | dry mustard powder
  - ¼ tsp | cayenne pepper
  - 3 cups | sharp cheddar, shredded off the block
  - 1 cup | Gruyère, shredded
  - 1 cup | fresh breadcrumbs
  - To taste | salt and pepper
method:
  - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the macaroni about 2 minutes short of al dente, then drain — it finishes cooking in the oven.
  - Melt 4 tbsp of the butter in a saucepan over medium heat, whisk in the flour, and cook until the roux smells toasty and turns pale gold, about 2 minutes.
  - Whisk in the milk gradually, then simmer, stirring often, until the sauce is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 8 minutes.
  - Off heat, stir in the mustard powder, cayenne, and both cheeses a handful at a time until fully melted and smooth.
  - Fold in the macaroni, season with salt and pepper, and pour into a buttered 9x13-inch baking dish.
  - Melt the remaining 2 tbsp butter, toss with the breadcrumbs, and scatter evenly over the top.
  - Bake at 375°F until the sauce bubbles at the edges and the crumbs are deep golden, 20 to 25 minutes.
  - Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving so the sauce sets enough to hold a scoop.
---

The béchamel is what separates this from the stuff in a box — it holds the cheese in suspension instead of letting it break into grease and clumps the second it cools. Shred the cheddar off a block yourself; pre-shredded bags are coated in starch to stop clumping in the bag, and that same coating stops the cheese from melting smooth here.

Gruyère brings a nutty depth that cheddar alone doesn't get to, but a good melting Gouda works if that's what's in the fridge. You can assemble the whole dish through the breadcrumb step a day ahead and refrigerate it — add about 10 minutes to the bake time to account for the cold start.
