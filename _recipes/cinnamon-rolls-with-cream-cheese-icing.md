---
title: Cinnamon Rolls with Cream Cheese Icing
date: 2026-08-01 17:00:02 -0400
description: An enriched dough rolled with brown sugar and cinnamon, proofed overnight in the fridge so morning is just a bake and an icing.
image: /assets/images/Cinnamon-Rolls-With-Cream-Cheese-Icing.webp
servings: 12
prep_time: 30 min (plus overnight rise)
cook_time: 30 min
tags: [bread, baking, breakfast, vegetarian, slow, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1 cup | whole milk, warmed to 110°F
  - 2¼ tsp | active dry yeast (1 packet)
  - ½ cup | granulated sugar
  - 6 tbsp | unsalted butter, melted, plus more for the dish
  - 2 | large eggs
  - 1 tsp | fine salt
  - 4½ cups | all-purpose flour, plus more for the counter
  - ¾ cup | packed brown sugar
  - 2 tbsp | ground cinnamon
  - 6 tbsp | unsalted butter, softened
  - 4 oz | cream cheese, softened
  - 4 tbsp | unsalted butter, softened
  - 1 cup | powdered sugar
  - ½ tsp | vanilla extract
  - 2 tbsp | whole milk, to thin
  - Pinch | fine salt, for the icing
method:
  - Whisk the warm milk, yeast, and a pinch of the granulated sugar together and let stand until foamy on top, about 5 minutes.
  - Whisk in the remaining granulated sugar, melted butter, eggs, and salt.
  - Add the flour and knead by hand or with a dough hook until smooth, elastic, and pulling cleanly from the sides of the bowl, about 8 minutes.
  - Transfer to an oiled bowl, cover, and let rise at room temperature until doubled, about 1 hour.
  - Punch down the dough and roll it out on a floured counter into a 16-by-12-inch rectangle.
  - Spread the softened butter over the dough, then sprinkle evenly with the brown sugar and cinnamon, pressing gently so it sticks.
  - Roll into a tight log from the long edge and pinch the seam closed.
  - Slice into 12 rolls with a piece of dental floss or a sharp serrated knife.
  - Arrange cut-side up in a buttered 9-by-13-inch baking dish, cover, and refrigerate overnight, 12 to 18 hours.
  - The next morning, set the dish out to come to room temperature while the oven heats to 350°F, about 30 minutes.
  - Bake until deep golden brown and the centers spring back when pressed, 25 to 30 minutes.
  - Whisk the cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, vanilla, milk, and a pinch of salt together until smooth, then spread over the rolls while they're still warm.
---

The overnight cold rise does two things at once — it fits the whole project around a normal bedtime instead of a 5 a.m. start, and the slow proof builds more flavor into the dough than the same hour at room temperature would. Pull the dish straight from the fridge to the counter; don't skip the warm-up, or the centers bake before the outside catches up.

Ice them warm, not hot — spread it the moment they come out and it half-melts into the cracks; wait until they're fully cool and it just sits on top like frosting on a cake, which isn't the same thing.
