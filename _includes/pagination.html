{%- comment -%} The links between pages of a recipe list. Usage: {% include pagination.html pagination=page.pagination label="Recipe pages" %} Renders nothing at all when everything fits on one page, which is the case for every tag today — the markup should appear when there is somewhere to go, not sit there greyed out on a list of nine. Numbered links rather than only Newer/Older, because a crawler follows links and will not click "Older" forty times: every page of the list is one hop from every other. The full range is printed with no ellipsis, which is right while the count is small; at one recipe a day this reaches ten pages around 2028, and that is the point to window it. "Newer" and "Older" rather than "Previous" and "Next": the lists are sorted by date, so that is what the two directions actually mean. They stay in the markup as spans when there is nowhere to go, so the numbers keep their place on the row instead of sliding sideways between pages. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign pages = include.pagination -%} {%- if pages.total_pages > 1 %}
